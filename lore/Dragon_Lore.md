# Dragon Lore — Living Canon

*Recovered from the original Vixen-era reference and revised with Alice and
Sable on 2026-08-29. Update as lore emerges through writing.*

## Purpose and Authority

This file establishes Dragonbound's dragon worldbuilding and overrides the
minimal ACOTAR canon on dragons. Its inspirations include *Eragon*, *Fourth
Wing*, and *How to Train Your Dragon*, but the rules below belong to this AU.

The file distinguishes four kinds of information:

- **On-page canon:** already established in prose.
- **Locked truth:** authorial truth that characters may not know yet.
- **In-world theory:** a belief or interpretation that may be incomplete.
- **Open:** deliberately unresolved and discovered through writing.

Alice writes Aurelie and Prince exclusively. This file describes their nature;
it does not grant another writer authority over their dialogue, actions,
thoughts, feelings, or choices.

---

## I. Core Premise — The First Rider Bond

**Locked truth: Aurelie and Prince are the first dragon and rider.**

No dragon has ever bonded with another creature in the way Prince bonded with
Aurelie. Dragons have mated with dragons and, in distant history, taken Fae
lovers while shifted. Neither is a rider bond. There is no precedent, ritual,
book, elder, or inherited framework that can explain what Aurelie and Prince
are becoming.

This means:

- Aurelie does not know what she is becoming.
- Prince has instinct and fragmentary ancestral memory, but no ancestral model
  for the bond itself.
- Every Fae who learns the truth will encounter an aberration, miracle, threat,
  or opportunity rather than a known magical category.
- Even beings with fragments of dragon lore can offer only partial history and
  speculation about the bond.

Prince experiences the bond as *right*. He does not need a theory to believe
that choosing Aurelie was correct.

## II. What Dragons Are

### Age and Cosmology

**Locked truth:** Dragons predate the present Cauldron-shaped order of
Prythian—its Courts, borders, Fae institutions, and modern magical taxonomy.
Whether they predate the Cauldron in an ultimate creation-myth sense remains
unresolved. Do not use dragon lore to settle ACOTAR's deepest cosmology unless
Alice and Sable decide it in play.

Dragons are not Made objects, Fae beasts, or servants of Court magic. Their
magic belongs to an older layer of the world.

### Dragons Shaped Prythian

**Locked truth:** The elemental regions existed before the Courts because
dragons made them.

For ages, dragons of particular affinities inhabited the same territories.
Their magic seeped into land, weather, water, stone, and living things until the
regions took on enduring elemental natures. Ice dragons did not belong to the
Winter Court; their long habitation helped create the land that later became
Winter. The same is true of the other six regions.

Modern Court magic therefore carries the geological memory of dragons. Courts
are later political structures built upon dragon-shaped land.

Prince's ice affinity gives him a deep resonance with Winter. He is drawn to
the land as kin-shaped territory and may regard Kallias, its present steward,
above rulers of other Courts. This is affinity and esteem, not nationality,
fealty, or submission. Alice decides how Prince expresses it on the page.

### The Origin of Daemati Bloodlines

**Locked truth, unknown in-world:** Dragon mind-touch is the source from which
Daemati ability ultimately descends.

Mature dragons once learned humanoid shapes and sometimes took Fae lovers.
Children from those unions carried diluted traces of dragon mind-touch. The
trait persisted most strongly in old and powerful Fae bloodlines, eventually
becoming what modern Prythian calls Daemati power.

Dragons are now myth, so no living character knows this history as fact. Amren,
Prison beings, ancient records, or ancestral impressions may hold fragments.

**Open:** If Prince ever chooses to speak mind-to-mind with Rhysand, either may
recognise an uncanny echo in the other. Prince might perceive something
dragon-shaped in Rhysand's power—or simply find the resemblance superficial.
Rhysand's wings add a visual rhyme, not proof of kinship.

### Where the Other Dragons Are

**Locked truth:** Dragons survive in a hidden pocket reality whose portal has
been lost, sealed, or forgotten by Prythian.

They are wild and have never formed rider bonds. Prince may therefore appear
unprecedented or altered to his own kind. Their reaction is open: curiosity,
reverence, fear, hostility, acceptance, or something stranger are all possible.
Do not frame rejection or being “tainted” as the default answer.

Aurelie's original mission—to deliver Prince to his kind—is theoretically
possible but practically formidable. The portal must be found, entered, and
survived; the dragons must then decide what Aurelie and Prince mean to them.

### Lifespan

**Locked truth, unknown and presently unverifiable:**

- Wild dragons live for millennia. Eventually their power and consciousness
  become too vast for the physical plane; their bodies dissolve into stardust
  and they join the cosmos.
- The rider bond pulls Prince's lifespan downward and Aurelie's upward until
  they settle into a shared span of several centuries.
- Neither knows this. Prince saved Aurelie from an ordinary mortal lifespan;
  Aurelie anchors Prince away from eventual cosmic dissolution.

No known character possesses a test or precedent capable of proving this.

## III. Growth and Maturation

Dragons grow extremely quickly at first, like solitary predators whose young
must become mobile and formidable early. Physical size does not imply equal
magical control, judgment, elemental development, or emotional experience.

Prince's on-page growth establishes the curve:

- At hatching: cat-sized.
- At roughly two weeks: large-cat-sized and barely fitting in a backpack.
- At roughly four weeks: comparable to Eris's hounds at the withers.
- At roughly six weeks / the current Chapter Two period: horse-sized.
- Through the following months: still growing dramatically, but the rate begins
  to taper.
- Around one year: near adult height.
- Around two to three years: full adult mass, strength, and magical maturity.

Prince grew more slowly in the magic-starved mortal lands, then entered a period
of violent catch-up growth after crossing into Prythian and absorbing ambient
magic. Future growth is discovered through play; do not assign a daily increase
or force him to reach a plot-required size on a schedule.

Use relational descriptions whenever possible. If a numeric measurement is
needed, specify whether it means shoulder height, nose-to-rump body length
(excluding tail), or wingspan. “Ten-foot dragon” is otherwise ambiguous.

## IV. Elemental Affinity

The dragons who survive in the hidden world descend predominantly from the
Aerial body lineage, which displaced other old physical lineages. Elemental
affinity is not a modern breed or Court nationality; it is an individual
dragon's oldest magical orientation.

| Later Court | Dragon affinity | Lasting imprint on the land |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | Poisonous fog | Vegetation, pollen, plant-derived toxins |
| Summer | Water | Sea, pressure, rain, steam |
| Autumn | Fire | Flame, heat, harvest, fallen-leaf colour |
| Winter | Ice | Frost, cold, snow, preserved stillness |
| Day | Sunlight | Solar heat, radiance, blinding light |
| Night | Moonlight | Silver-cold lunar power, shadowed terrain |
| Dawn | Stardust | Cosmic substance and the dragons' final ascent |

Colour often signals affinity, with natural variation:

- Spring: green, pink, brown, yellow.
- Summer: sea-blue, sand-yellow, white.
- Autumn: red, orange, gold, brown.
- Winter: white, ice-blue, silver.
- Day: gold, white, bronze.
- Night: black, deep blue, silver.
- Dawn: pearl, opalescent, soft pink, pale gold.

**Locked truth:** Prince has an ice affinity. He does not yet know this and has
not manifested an elemental breath weapon on-page. Its first appearance must be
earned through writing rather than tied to a fixed age.

## V. The Dragon–Rider Bond

### Established and Developing Effects

- **Private telepathy:** emotions first, then images, then words. Prince and
  Aurelie now communicate in full thoughts and sentences.
- **Emotional sharing:** they sense one another's moods, fear, joy, hunger, and
  pain.
- **Sensory crossover:** Prince has perceived through Aurelie's senses. Further
  control and reciprocity develop through play.
- **Shared lifespan:** locked truth described above, unknown to them.
- **Magic channeling:** Aurelie will eventually be able to channel magic through
  Prince.
- **Physical transformation:** the bond is gradually making Aurelie something
  new: dragon-touched and Rider-Made, not simply Fae.

### Two Kinds of Mind-Touch

The rider bond is private, continuous, and intimate. It is broadly analogous to
mindspeech through a mating bond but specifically scoped to dragon and rider.
No bystander—including Eris—hears untranslated exchanges.

Prince also possesses the broader ancestral ability to touch other minds,
similar to Daemati contact. This is deliberate access he may offer or withhold,
not an ambient broadcast. He has refused to speak directly to Eris at the
current cursor, making Aurelie his necessary translator during lessons.

Characters may react only to visible behaviour, spoken translations, or magic
they can independently sense. Mindspeech is formatted in italics:
*"Like this."*

### Severability

**Locked truth:** The bond cannot be severed while both live. Death is the only
known endpoint.

Faebane can dampen the bond without destroying it. While Aurelie wears the
bracelets, words and presence become inaccessible or severely muted; both
return when the faebane is removed.

## VI. Faebane, Made Objects, and Leverage

### Faebane

- **Prince:** Faebane does not directly suppress his magic. A faebane collar on
  him would be useless as a magical restraint.
- **Aurelie:** Faebane affects her increasingly as the bond transforms her. It
  dampens the bond, suppresses emerging magic, and leaves her physically close
  to mortal fragility.

Aurelie is therefore the available point of leverage. Captivity, threatened
harm, or faebane used against her may influence Prince because he loves and is
bound to her. This does **not** give the captor ownership of their bond or direct
command over Prince.

### Made Objects

**Locked limit:** Prince's older magic gives him strong resistance to Made
objects, but the interaction is untested. Do not assume the Crown, Mask, Harp,
or another Made object can command him; do not assume absolute immunity either.
The first collision should remain dangerous and capable of surprising everyone.

Objects or forces older than the current Cauldron-shaped order may exist and
could affect dragons more directly. These are open plot possibilities, not
prewritten answers.

## VII. Why Shielding Is Difficult for Dragons

Prince's difficulty with shielding is not a deficit of power, intelligence, or
capacity. It is a mismatch between dragon nature and a technique developed by
beings whose survival depended upon concealment and containment.

When dragons roamed Prythian, they had no biological or magical need to become
smaller, quieter, or less perceptible. Presence was protection. Their magic was
meant to exist at full volume, and the land itself moved around them.

A dragon therefore has no native reflex corresponding to a Fae child's shield.
Prince must invent magical self-containment against the grain of his body,
magic, and ancestral inheritance. The useful analogy is a wolf being asked to
perform tricks like a domesticated dog: the difficulty lies in the demand, not
the wolf's capability.

A functional moving shield must eventually adhere to the self like another
layer of scale rather than remain anchored around a location. How Prince learns
that bodily boundary, how reliably he can reproduce it, and how long it takes
remain live RP developments. Do not assign a fixed success date, linear
timetable, or mandatory failure mode.

## VIII. Aurelie's Transformation

### Slow Phase

Magic emerges before obvious physical change: lighting candles, moving small
objects, minor illusions, or similarly small manifestations. The phase is
gradual and concealable. Aurelie may not initially understand what is happening.

At first she has no innate mental shield. Her mind is vulnerable to Daemati
intrusion. Prince may sense intrusion before he learns how—or whether—to extend
protection around her.

### Imbuement Trigger

**Locked future truth:** During mortal danger, Aurelie chooses Eris and Prince
imbues her with his magic out of necessity. That act triggers the rapid phase of
her transformation. The precise danger, choices, and scene remain unwritten and
must be earned in play. The Fae mating bond with Eris manifests in the same
crisis.

### Rapid Phase

Over the following weeks Aurelie becomes the first of her kind: not High Fae,
but dragon-touched and Rider-Made.

Possible developments include sharper senses, faster reflexes, longer ears,
subtly elongated canines, dragon-like changes in the eyes, and other physical
signs discovered through writing.

After the transformation she gains one specific signet or gift arising from
her own nature in combination with the bond. The gift is **open** and must not
be predetermined by a reference file.

## IX. Prince's Knowledge and Instincts

### What He Knows by Instinct

- He is a dragon.
- He can touch minds and communicate through the rider bond.
- He can hunt prey appropriate to his size.
- The bond with Aurelie is right.
- His magic is supposed to be large, present, and uncontained.

### Ancestral Fragments

- Images and responses belonging to situations he has never personally lived.
- Occasional names, words, or concepts without context.
- A sense of a home he cannot locate.
- Recognition or reverence around some ancient magical signatures, including
  the cosmic substance dragons eventually become.

### What He Does Not Yet Know

- That he is the first dragon to form a rider bond.
- His ice affinity or future breath weapon.
- The lifespan exchange.
- The full shape of Aurelie's transformation.
- How to shield himself reliably or protect Aurelie's mind.
- How to find the hidden dragon world.

He may feel that he and Aurelie are unprecedented without possessing the fact
that no earlier rider bond existed.

## X. Mating and Reproduction

- Dragons mate for life.
- Egg-laying is rare: usually two or three eggs in a clutch, with decades or
  centuries between clutches.
- Whether Prince's rider bond affects his ability to mate or sire offspring is
  open.
- Prince does not lay eggs. This is not an open thread.

## XI. Shapeshifting and Mimicry

### The Ability

**Locked truth:** Mature dragons can learn to shapeshift into humanoid or other
creature forms. It is learned mimicry, not an automatic instinct.

A dragon must observe the target form over time and understand its anatomy,
movement, balance, and magical feel. Months or years of attention may be needed
before a convincing transformation is possible.

### The Cost

Holding another shape consumes concentration and magic. The longer the dragon
remains shifted, the greater the exhaustion, instability, sensory confusion,
and risk of an uncontrolled reversion. Rest and time in true form are required
afterward.

Young or inexperienced dragons may manage only brief transformations. There is
no fixed 24-hour cliff and no rule that a dragon permanently becomes the copied
creature. Limits develop through experience and are discovered in play.

### Prince

Prince is too young to shift now and will likely require years of observation
and maturation. He may possess ancestral fragments of the concept without any
practical method.

His eventual shifted appearance, preferred forms, skill, and reasons for using
them are open. The mechanic exists partly to support later stories in this AU
that need not centre Aurelie, but it creates no assumed romantic or sexual path
between Prince and Aurelie.

Managing the costs of shifting is Prince's decision and a risk Prince and
Aurelie may navigate together—not authority Aurelie possesses over his body.

## XII. Vulnerabilities

- Dragons are powerful, not invincible. Sufficient violence can kill them;
  magic can wound them.
- Prince's size and magical presence make secrecy progressively harder.
- Faebane can isolate and weaken Aurelie, creating indirect leverage over him.
- Aurelie remains vulnerable to Daemati intrusion until she develops a shield
  or Prince learns to extend protection.
- Prince strongly resists Made magic, but no one knows the limits.
- Shielding asks dragons to suppress their most natural defence: overwhelming
  presence.
- Ancient objects, hostile dragons, or forces outside modern Fae knowledge may
  present vulnerabilities nobody can currently predict.

## XIV. Eris's Knowledge Ledger

### At First Encounter

Eris could deduce:

- Prince was ancient in nature and older than familiar Fae categories.
- Prince and Aurelie were bonded.
- Prince was magically powerful and broadcasting an unmistakable signature.
- His rapid growth made secrecy temporary.

Eris did **not** know:

- What dragons specifically were or what they could do.
- Whether the bond could be severed.
- The lifespan exchange, transformation, affinity system, hidden world, or
  first-rider status.

### Current Chapter Two Cursor

Eris now knows through observation that:

- Prince and Aurelie communicate privately and Aurelie translates Prince's
  meaning.
- Faebane on Aurelie dampens their contact while leaving Prince's magic active.
- Prince's magical presence can be trained toward containment, though the act
  is profoundly unnatural and progress is unreliable.
- A stationary boundary fails when Prince moves unless it is taught to adhere
  to his body.
- Prince is intelligent, politically aware, hostile to Eris, and capable of
  cooperating when freedom or Aurelie's safety gives him reason.

Eris still does **not** know:

- That this is history's first rider bond.
- The lifespan exchange or Rider-Made transformation.
- Prince's ice affinity or elemental future.
- The hidden dragon world or the truth of Daemati ancestry.
- The absolute limits of faebane, Made objects, shielding, or control.
- Whether Prince can speak directly into other minds; Prince has deliberately
  withheld that contact from him.

### Eris's Rational Calculations

- Direct command of Prince is unproven and likely dangerous to attempt.
- Aurelie can be constrained, threatened, bargained with, or exposed to
  faebane, creating leverage without ownership.
- The bargain is his strongest existing instrument because it is binding and
  operates through Court magic he understands.
- Prince's growth and magical volume create political time pressure.
- Long-term control may be impossible even if short-term leverage works.
