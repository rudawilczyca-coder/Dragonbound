## Dragon–Rider Mindspeech

- Prince and Aurelie communicate mind to mind through their bond. In this AU,
  the mechanism is broadly analogous to mindspeech through a mating bond, but
  it is specifically scoped to dragon and rider.
- Their private mindspeech is not audible to Eris or any other observer.
  Characters may react only to visible behaviour, spoken translations, or
  magic they can independently sense.
- Prince can deliberately initiate direct mind-to-mind communication with
  beings other than Aurelie, similar to Rhys's daemati contact. This is a
  conscious choice, not an ambient broadcast.
- Prince has refused to speak directly to Eris up to the current Chapter 2
  cursor. Aurelie is therefore essential to shielding lessons because she
  translates Prince's words for Eris. Do not let Eris hear or infer the exact
  content of untranslated exchanges.
- Mindspeech is formatted in italics: *"Like this."*
