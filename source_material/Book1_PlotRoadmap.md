## 🎯 Core Engines (The Through-Lines)

### The Plot Engine
Eris is mid-coup against Beron. He needs four courts to back his claim plus enough Autumn nobility for a clean transfer. Aurelie and Prince are the leverage that destabilizes Beron's grip. The court tour serves dual purpose — she gathers dragon information, he gathers political alliances. The bargain binds them together until Prince is fully shielded and the courts have been visited.

### The Heart Engine
Eris's wound is Beron — five centuries of being built into an instrument of cruelty. He cannot soften without his identity collapsing. Aurelie's wound is her erasure — raised to be a vessel, not a person. They circle each other in mutual armor and mutual hunger.

### The Body Engine — The Dual Register
The cruelty IS the body plot. His anger arouses her; her defiance arouses him. Every threat scene is also a desire scene. Neither can address one without exposing the other. The wanting hides inside the violence until they run out of violence to hide behind.

### The Political Engine
Aurelie falls in love with Autumn's people before she falls in love with Autumn's heir. Tomas, Una, Briar, the lesser fae, the border settlements ground under Beron's boot. She fights for Eris's throne not for him — for them. The political investment precedes the personal one and the gap between them is its own story.

### The Mate Bond Engine
The bond is forming throughout, invisible, unconfirmed. It will manifest fully at the trigger event when Prince imbues Aurelie with his power. Until then it operates as undertow — pulling them toward each other against their conscious wishes.

## 📅 Chapter-by-Chapter Roadmap

---

### **CHAPTER 1 — The Meeting** *(written)*
Aurelie meets Eris at the stream in former Spring territory. Three days' travel north. Arrival at Ashgrove. The bargain is struck. The bracelets are fitted. Aurelie promises to murder Eris when the bargain is fulfilled. He acknowledges the threat with the courtesy of a man who takes it seriously.

**Ends:** Late August. Day 6 at Ashgrove.

---

### **CHAPTERS 2–3 — The Settling & The Tightening** *(planned in detail — see Chapter2_3_DayMap.md)*

30 days at Ashgrove. September 15 → October 15.

**Cruelty escalation in three movements:**
- Type 1 (controlled, surgical) — Days 1-15
- Type 3 (the open-door interrogation) — Day 21
- Type 2 (the volatile state, the throat-grab) — Day 29

**Aurelie's awakening:**
- Meets the household — Tomas, Briar, Caius softening
- Discovers Beron's border settlement atrocities in the library
- Privately decides to help Eris take the throne — for Autumn's people, not for him

**Body plot ignites:**
- Wall pin (Day 7): *"You smell like smoke when you're angry. Go on, then."*
- The Plate, the Aedric dinner, the dawn balcony, the throat-grab, the library silence
- Every cruelty annotated with its dual desire register

**Caelum:** Three landmarks of his frame straining. The dismissal threat is planted but not executed.

**Prince:** Achieves 90-second sustained shielding. Reveals new capability — telepathic intervention with minds other than Aurelie's.

**Ends:** Mid-October. The library silence. Both still standing.

---

### **CHAPTERS 4–5 — The Shift**

The instrument has broken. Eris stops trying to break her. She tells him she'll help — on her terms.

**Major beats:**
- **The Una speech.** Aurelie tells Eris she's helping for Tomas's daughter, not for him. *"Don't make it about you."* Devastating because Eris has owned Ashgrove for two centuries and never learned the daughter's name. Aurelie did in two months.
- **Active intelligence sharing begins.** She's *better at reading mortals and lesser fae than he is*. She destabilizes him by being useful in ways he didn't authorize.
- **The bracelets come off.** Strategic concession — he needs her bond with Prince at full strength. She does not thank him.
- **Eris's first involuntary kindness + immediate overcorrection.** Maybe the healer for Una's chest cold. Aurelie discovers it through Tomas. Eris punishes her for making him do it.
- **Caelum's continued presence.** *Not yet dismissed.* He is warming to her — small smiles, gentler corrections, the occasional touch that lingers a half-beat too long. The professional frame is buckling. Whether he leaves or stays is a Chapter 5-6 question, decided by how the dynamic develops.
- **Prince's shielding** — sustained for 5-10 minutes now. Approaching usable.

**Body plot:** The violence is off the table. The cordiality between Eris and Aurelie at private dinners is its own torture. They are performing politeness over a current that hums under everything.

**Ends:** Late October / early November. Preparation for the Inner Circle visit underway.

---

### **CHAPTERS 6–7 — The Inner Circle**

Aurelie meets the Night Court inner circle. Either Az visits Ashgrove first OR they go to Velaris.

**Major beats:**
- **Aurelie meets Mor, Rhys, Cassian, Azriel** (Feyre, Amren, Nesta possibly).
- **Mor approaches Aurelie privately and offers rescue if she ever wants it.** Aurelie does not accept. Does not refuse. Files it.
- **The yellow dress is planted but not detonated.** Mor sees something in Aurelie's bearing that reminds her of her own past — a half-recognition that doesn't fully resolve.
- **Aurelie discovers she has potential allies outside Ashgrove.** The terror has competition: hope is back on the table.
- **Eris is uncomfortable here.** The Inner Circle are the only people who might *see* him. He performs immaculately and Aurelie watches the performance strain.
- **The first near-miss touch.** Adjusting her sleeve before walking into a Night Court reception. Pulse, heat, fire flicker, neither moves. The body plot has now passed the heart plot in awareness.

**Ends:** Aurelie is shaken but not extracted. She's chosen — not consciously, not yet — to stay through the court tour. The Mor seed is planted to bloom later.

---

### **CHAPTERS 8–12 — The Court Tour**

🌅 **Day Court (Helion)** — *Easiest alliance.* Helion flirts (he flirts with everyone). Aurelie flirts back lightly because she is nineteen and starved for attention from beautiful men who are not Eris. Eris's reaction is catastrophic and he cannot convince himself it's strategic. Day signs on.

🌊 **Summer Court (Tarquin)** — Tarquin is *sincere*. Treats Aurelie as a political peer, asks substantive questions about the mortal lands. Eris finds this somehow worse than Helion's flirtation. Summer signals support pending dragon disclosure.

🌃 **Dawn Court (Thesan)** — Civilized. Polite. Brief. Useful intelligence-gathering on Aurelie's part — she's serious about dragon research now. Thesan is non-committal but warming. Eris is quieter post-Dawn — the cumulative strain shows. **The burns reveal:** one night, Aurelie comes to his rooms to discuss protocol; he answers the door with sleeves pushed up. She sees Beron's scars. She doesn't ask. He doesn't cover them.

❄️ **Winter Court (Kallias & Viviane)** — *The detonation.*
- **Prince speaks mind-to-mind with Kallias for the first time.** Kin to kin — something he won't offer any other High Lord.
- **Winter offers asylum specifically to Prince.** Not to Aurelie (though she would be welcome by extension). Eris is positioned as *warden, not patron*. Politically humiliating.
- **Prince's element is revealed: ice.** He learns his breath here.
- **Aurelie watches Viviane and Kallias** — a real marriage, equal, loving. She sees what a court can be when its rulers actually love each other. Her face betrays her wanting. Eris sees it.
- **THE FIRST KISS.** Post-dinner, in a corridor, after the asylum offer. Eris intercepts her still shaking from the political humiliation. He kisses her *furiously* — retaliation, not romance. **She kisses him back.** The body plot has been ahead of them for chapters and now the mouths know it. He says something cruel immediately. She doesn't flinch. She walks away. He is wrecked.

🌃 **Night Court (final visit)** — *The reckoning.*
- **The Mor flashback fully detonates.** Mor sees the dress in a memory or sensory trigger and approaches Aurelie privately. The conversation Aurelie has been preparing for since the corridor in Chapter 1.
- **Aurelie now KNOWS about Mor.** She has to decide what to do with it.
- **An Illyrian flirts with her.** Not Cassian/Az/Rhys (who are taken) but a regular Illyrian warrior — handsome, charming, offering her *one night she'd never forget*. She considers it. She refuses. Eris *smells her considering* and CANNOT contain the rage.
- **The fight that follows.** *"I am giving you a fucking THRONE and the moment I want something for myself, even for one night, you have to take it away?! You are more of a monster than your father!"* / *"You want something for yourself. Then fucking take it!"*
- **THEY FUCK.** Furious. Devastating. Both wrecked. *"I am still going to kill you."* / *"I expected nothing less."* Both lying. Both knowing the other is lying. **The new dynamic.**
- **The morning. The marks are gone.** The bargain has fulfilled. Aurelie is free.
- **THE BREAKFAST SCENE.** The knife on the table. *"I could kill you now."* / *"You could certainly try, my little dragon-keeper."* The fear in his eyes — real fear because she's powerful enough by then that he is not 100% certain of the outcome. She picks up the knife. Slices ham. *"There are parts of you I have use for, currently. If I ever get bored of them, I'll reconsider."* Asks about the day's schedule. Goes back to breakfast. **He is hard. He is afraid. He cannot reach for her without confirming he is hers. He sits and breathes through his nose and asks about carriages.**

**Ends:** Aurelie chooses to stay. Not as captive. As architect. The bargain is gone. She remains anyway. The book has just become a different book.

### **CHAPTERS 13–15 — The Trigger**

Beron's loyalists move first. The trap springs too early — Eris was not ready.

**The atrocity:** A Beron-loyalist faction strikes a noble house Eris was protecting. Casualties. Public exposure of Eris's "weakness" with the human girl.

**The realization:** Eris understands Beron knows about Aurelie. The strategic frame collapses — she is no longer leverage, she is *target*. He chooses her safety over coup-timing. He doesn't know yet that he's chosen her. He thinks he's protecting the asset.

**The body plot goes quiet.** They sleep in the same room because he won't leave her unguarded. They don't touch. The intimacy has gone somewhere harder than sex.

**The direct attack:** Beron's forces strike Ashgrove. Aurelie in mortal danger. Night Court intervenes (alliance pays off). Eris fights — volatile state surfaces fully, fire uncontrolled, kills with visible emotion.

**THE IMBUEMENT.** In the moment of mortal danger, Aurelie chooses Eris — throws herself between him and a blade, calls Prince's protection toward him rather than herself. Prince imbues her in this moment. The necessity is the love, not the violence. **She begins transforming.** Eris watches her *become*.

**THE MATING BOND.** Snaps into place. Both can feel it. Neither knows how long it's been there.

**The succession crisis:** Beron's failure to defend Autumn's interests is publicly exposed. The Inner Circle is *seen* protecting Aurelie. Beron is shown to be impotent or complicit. The courts move.

---

### **CHAPTERS 16+ — The Throne**

Eris becomes High Lord of Autumn. Beron contained, dead, or exiled (TBD).

**The reckoning:** The bargain is long gone. The mating bond is named. Aurelie is transforming — physically, magically — into something the playbook has no entry for.

**Eris must learn to live in a body that can be read by someone who loves him.** The privacy that protected him for 500 years is gone. The mate bond is sensory.

**Aurelie must build the Autumn Court she imagined when she walked through Velaris.** Not Velaris — *Autumn's version*. Fire and forest and old stone, but with the dignity she promised herself she would wrestle out of Eris if she had to.

**The murder threat still stands. Technically.** Whether she ever rescinds it is its own arc.

**Caelum's fate, the Lucien reconciliation, the Inner Circle's permanent place in their lives — all open questions.** Book One ends with the throne secured and the work of building beginning. Book Two's territory.

## 🔑 Locked-In Anchors (Do Not Lose These)

### Tonal Anchors
- The cruelty is real. Eris means it. Aurelie endures it without breaking but without being invincible.
- The body plot runs underneath the violence. It does not need separate scenes.
- Caelum is kindness Aurelie does not fully reciprocate as romance. He is the door that opens the heart for someone *else*.
- Prince is not a sidekick. He is ancient, sentient, possessive, dryly funny, and possibly the most intelligent character in the room at any time.
- Aurelie's transformation is *earned*. Every choice, every refusal to break, every act of defiance and quiet investment is the soil it grows from.

### Structural Anchors
- **The bargain dissolves at the Night Court visit.** Sex first, marks gone the next morning. Aurelie chooses to stay.
- **The first kiss is at Winter Court.** The first sex is at Night Court.
- **The breakfast scene is locked.** *"I could kill you now."* The knife. The ham. The cock line (Option 3 — *"There are parts of you I have use for, currently."*). It is the inversion of the entire previous dynamic and the moment Aurelie steps into queenship.
- **The trigger event is the imbuement.** Aurelie transforms during a mortal-danger moment in which she chooses Eris. The mating bond manifests then.
- **The Una speech happens before the Inner Circle visit.** It's why she's helping at all.
- **The Mor reckoning happens at the Night Court visit, not before.** The dress is planted in Chapter 1. It detonates in Chapter 12-ish.

### Character Anchors
- **Eris does not bluff.** Threats made are threats kept.
- **Eris does not soften gracefully.** Every crack is involuntary and immediately overcorrected.
- **Aurelie does not become Fae through external magic.** Prince imbues her. The dragon's choice. The transformation is a gift, not a curse.
- **The lesser fae conversion is the political spine.** Tomas, Una, Briar, Caius. They are why Aurelie stays.
- **Velaris is the proof of concept.** What a court can be when its High Lord chooses to be different. Aurelie sees it and decides to make Autumn its own version.

### Body Plot Anchors
- **"You smell like smoke when you're angry. Go on, then."** — Day 7 wall pin.
- **The Plate scene** — humiliation that becomes mutual destabilization.
- **The throat-grab** — the body keeping score for the Winter kiss.
- **The Winter kiss** — retaliation, not romance. She kisses him back.
- **The Night Court fuck** — *"I still want to kill you." / "I expected nothing less."*
- **The breakfast** — the inversion. The wheel turns.
