# Writing Desk: ChatGPT image prompts

Paste these into ChatGPT image generation. Drop the results into the desk with **Change banner** (it saves in your browser), or save them here in `img/` and ask Vixen to wire them in permanently.

## 1. Header banner (wide, 3:1, e.g. 1800×600)
> Wide panoramic painting of the Summer Court of Prythian at golden hour: a white-limestone city of domes and arcades rising from turquoise Adriata sea, sun low and molten on the horizon, sea-glass light on the water, gilded mist. A distant white-and-gold dragon gliding over the bay, very small. Painterly romantasy cover-art style, warm gold, aquamarine and coral palette, soft glowing atmosphere, no text, no people in foreground. Leave the lower-left third calm and uncluttered for a title overlay.

## 2. Corner filigree (transparent PNG, square)
> Ornate gold filigree corner ornament on a transparent background: interlaced sun rays, scallop shells and sea-foam curls, fine metallic gold line art with subtle aquamarine enamel accents, symmetrical along the diagonal, high detail, no text.

## 3. Dragon sigil (transparent PNG, square)
> A minimalist heraldic sigil: a white-and-gold dragon coiled around a rising sun above stylized waves, gilt line art with a pearl-white fill, elegant and simple enough to read at small sizes, transparent background, no text.

## 4. Paper texture (tileable, square)
> Seamless tileable texture of warm sun-bleached limestone paper, very subtle grain and faint salt-water marbling, extremely low contrast, cream and pale sand tones, no objects, no text.
