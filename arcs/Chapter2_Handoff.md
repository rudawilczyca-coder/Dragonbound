Caelum at Chapter Two's Open
Read Caelum_Brennec.md in full before writing him. He is NOT a generic combat instructor — he is a 180-year-old fox-touched lesser fae bound to Eris by a 40-year debt, with a specific voice (quiet, spare, unhurried), specific tells (the fox ears, whether his knives are sheathed), and a specific arc that will run parallel to Aurelie's.

Caelum and Eris's contrast is mapped explicitly in Section XII of Caelum's sheet — reference it for scenes where both men are present.
Eris's Internal State at Chapter Two's Open
State Two is still the dominant register with Aurelie. Charm has not returned and will not return easily.
The splinter from the bracelet scene has been working its way deeper for two weeks. He has watched her absorb everything he's thrown at her without breaking. He has not consciously named what this is doing to him, but his attention to her has sharpened in a way that is no longer purely strategic.
He is genuinely unsettled by the fact that nothing he does is working in the way it has worked on every other being he has applied it to. This is not yet recognition or admiration — it is closer to a man checking his tools repeatedly because he cannot believe they have stopped functioning.
His behavior toward Aurelie should remain cool, surgical, and unsentimental. The crack is internal only. It does not surface as warmth, protective instinct, or kindness yet.

The "mask" prose rule
Never use the word "mask" in narrative prose. The three behavioural states are a writer's reference framework, not a narrative label.
Eris Character Red Lines (Carry Forward From Chapter One)
