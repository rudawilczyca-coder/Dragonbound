Timeline Placement
Post-ACOSF, approximately 5–6 months after the events of that book end.
~2.5 years after the Wall fell during the Hybern War (ACOWAR).
Current season: Late August / very beginning of September. End of summer tipping toward autumn.
Prologue covered Aurelie's flight from Hightower, her sea voyage on the Tern's Wager, her overland travel through former Spring Court territory, and her crossing into Prythian.
Chapter One opens approximately 3–4 days after Aurelie crosses into Prythian.
What Happened in Chapter One
The Meeting (Day 1, midday)
Eris tracked an ancient, unshielded magical signature from beyond Autumn's borders for days before riding out personally with his foxhounds. He found Aurelie at a stream in former Spring Court territory — filthy, dagger-in-hand, with a small dragon perched on her shoulder. His hounds prostrated themselves in the dragon's presence; Prince accepted the deference and then released them as a gesture of goodwill.
Aurelie, green to Prythian and operating on instinct, asked Eris if he was Tamlin. He found this insulting on multiple overlapping levels and filed it as evidence of how little she knew. He introduced himself as Eris Vanserra, heir to the Autumn Court — a name that meant nothing to her. She gave her name as "Lilly." He clocked the lie immediately but did not press it.
Aurelie agreed to travel north with Eris under the false belief that he would help her find dragons or knowledge of dragons. Prince advised her not to give her real name or his.
The Road North (Days 1–3)
Three days of riding through ungoverned territory toward Eris's private estate, Ashgrove.
Aurelie's body was unprepared for sustained riding; her legs deteriorated badly by Day 2.
Prince refused the backpack and grew steadily larger, gaining inches between encounter and arrival. By Day 3 he was as large as Eris's hounds at the withers.
Prince told Aurelie, in defense of Tamlin: "Even the strongest men can be broken by love, but it is better to be broken than never to have loved at all." (Eris noted this; Eris filed it without comment.)
Close encounter on Day 3 with a Summer Court patrol — Prince hid himself, dampening his magical signature for the first time without understanding how. The Summer scouts informed Eris of an "unusual magical signature" they had been sent to investigate. Eris redirected them south.
Eris began assembling a profile of who Aurelie really was: noble household, Hightower port city, betrothed to Ismena's middle son, fluent etiquette reflexes, a mother who had clearly trained her in negotiation. He never confronted her with these deductions on the road.
Aurelie's first real hit on the road: she connected Eris's lecture about Tamlin ("power and love make dangerous bedfellows") to her own sense that Eris's history might be similar — though she did not voice this aloud yet.
Arrival at Ashgrove (Day 3, evening)
Eris's private estate, distinct from any official Autumn Court holding. Staffed by trusted lesser fae who report to Eris, not Beron. Caius, the steward (60+ years in Eris's service), met them at the threshold and only briefly lost composure at the sight of the dragon.
Aurelie was given the green room in the east wing. Prince claimed the bed instantly. A bath was drawn. A lesser fae girl named Briar attended Aurelie and ran from the dragon initially.
Aurelie, in the absence of any clean clothing of her own, opened a trunk in the green room and took a yellow dress with walnut-brown lacing that fit her well enough. Briar — only two years in service, no historical context — assumed the dress was simply old and unfashionable and did not stop her.
The Corridor (Day 3, before dinner)
After fighting with Prince over whether they should trust Eris (Prince: not at all; Aurelie: he is offering food and shelter for free), Aurelie stormed out of the bedroom and collided with Eris in the hallway.
The dress had belonged to Mor — from when she was promised to Eris, before she chose Cassian and her family destroyed her. The trunk had not been opened in years. Eris recognized it immediately.
His State Two slipped briefly. The corridor sconces flared. He told her, voice raw: "Take it off. There are clothes being sent to your room. Wear those. That dress is not yours. It was never meant to be yours. And I will not see it worn at my table."
He walked away. He did not explain. He locked himself in his study, burned through two logs of his own hearth-fire, and reassembled himself before dinner.
Aurelie connected the moment to the Tamlin lecture and concluded that "power and love make dangerous bedfellows" applied to him, too. She had no further specifics — she did not know about Mor.
The First Dinner & The Bargain (Day 3, evening)
Eris arrived composed. He delivered a controlled, sideways apology — explaining only that the dress had belonged to "someone promised to him, long ago" and that the arrangement had ended badly. He moved past it.
Eris laid out three terms for the visit:
Aurelie remains at Ashgrove indefinitely until he decides her companion's existence can be revealed on his terms.
She does not contact any other court without his knowledge.
The dragon learns to shield.
Aurelie countered:
Prince may leave the lodge but must remain within a 1-mile perimeter; once he can shield, his movements are his own.
Eris will arrange for visits to other courts when politically possible, so they can search for other dragons.
Eris agreed. Added one clause of his own: she would not lie to him directly. Omission and refusal he would accept; fabrication he would not.
Aurelie raised her glass and said: "We have an accord."
The bargain marks formed instantly — a curling line of red-gold flame on her left forearm and his right, twin to each other, mirrored to align if their wrists ever met. (Design: "Ember Script" — flowing, unreadable script in molten gold-red, like ink written in fire, fading from whisper-thin at the wrist to drifting embers at the elbow.)
Eris explained Fae bargain magic only AFTER the mark formed. He had withheld the information deliberately. He framed his disclosure as honesty: he had not lied; she had simply not asked. The bargain is unbreakable except by fulfillment of terms or death of one party. The mark binds him as completely as her — he cannot violate the concessions he made.
Aurelie's response: she threw the wine glass at his face, screamed "I hate you!" and ran upstairs to Prince, who had felt the magic seal in real time and was destroying furniture.
Eris finished his wine. Dismissed the staff. Read correspondence in his study. Went to bed without remorse.
Day 4 — Self-Imposed Isolation
Aurelie did not leave her room. Food was delivered on trays. She slept poorly, plagued by nightmares. Prince ate on the balcony at dawn.
Day 5 (Morning) — The Library
Aurelie asked Briar to show her to the library. The library was vast, chaotic, never properly catalogued — clearly a working library, used by Eris for centuries. He had left a note: "The east shelves are mine. Touch what you like. The locked cabinet is locked for reasons. — E."
Aurelie began a self-directed cataloguing project (her spite-energy turned productive). She read a textbook-level history of Prythian's seven courts and the Autumn Court's High Lord lineage, gathering basic intelligence on Beron and the political structure.
Day 5 (Evening) — Second Dinner
Aurelie came to dinner — initially planning to refuse, but pulled by Briar's evident fear of what would happen if the guest refused. She told Eris: "Briar is afraid of you." He responded coolly that Briar was afraid of him as everyone else in the household was, and that this was Aurelie's problem to manage if she chose to.
Aurelie probed his wound — "And as for the lesser fae being treated without kindness... I suppose you learned that from your father?" — testing for cracks.
Eris went still. Did not flare. Replied flat: "You've been reading. Competent enough as a record. Less competent as a weapon." Set the morning's shielding lesson for dawn. Walked out.
Day 6 — Shielding Training, Round 1
Prince attempted to find his magical "edge" by pulling inward toward a center. He could not. His description: "There is no end. There is me, and then at some point there is the outer world. I cannot find where I end and the outside begins."
Eris recognized the problem: Prince had no edge because he had never had one. Fae learn shielding as children because the boundary already exists. Prince was born continuous with the world. Eris pivoted: instead of finding the edge, find the center. Pull one thread of magic inward at a time. Compress, don't reach.
Aurelie asked if Eris also felt the world around him with his magic. He answered honestly. Then mentioned, almost in passing, that something was hunting them. She did not know. He listed the threats: Beron first, then every other High Lord, then mercenaries and lesser fae lords, then beings older than the courts. He told her: "You are not in danger from one thing. You are in danger from a list."
Aurelie was shaken but defended herself by snapping: "You replaced one leash for another and called it generosity." This was her first real hit on him — a sentence that landed exactly where it was aimed. He walked away from the argument rather than answer it, prioritizing Prince's lesson.
Day 6 (Evening) — Third Dinner & The Bracelets
Aurelie wore a dress with elbow-length sleeves and a sheer flared cuff that displayed the bargain mark openly. She had her hair down. She wanted Eris to see what he had done.
Eris matched her — pushed his own right sleeve back to display his matching mark. "Better. If we're going to have it between us, we may as well have it where we can see it."
Aurelie asked about his coup against Beron and how he intended to use them. Eris answered plainly, bound by the bargain not to fabricate: he needs four courts to back him, plus enough Autumn nobility to consolidate the transfer. Prince is leverage — his existence in Eris's territory shifts the calculation of every noble currently choosing between Eris and Beron. Aurelie herself will not be paraded; she is the bond, and the bond is the mechanism.
Eris also raised, almost casually, that Aurelie cannot fight. He proposed she train. She refused: "I am a lady, and ladies do not fight."
Then Aurelie crossed a line she did not understand she was crossing. She suggested — partially testing, partially probing — that Prince could simply go to Beron once he learned to shield and serve up Eris's coup on a silver platter.
State Two locked into place. Eris went still and cool. He revealed, plainly, that he had known her real identity since the second night on the road: Lord and Lady Delacroix of Hightower, three brothers (Bastien, Emile, Henri), maidservant Lilly, betrothal to Queen Ismena's middle son, the egg catalogued as item #312 in Ismena's family ledger. He had not used the information. He had not threatened her family. He had not sent a single message south.
He then made the threat explicit: if Prince ever brought intelligence on the coup to Beron, every member of her family in Hightower would be dead within four days. The bargain protected Aurelie. It did not protect them.
Aurelie was sick. Prince's mental voice, exhausted: "You can't outmaneuver him. You can't fight him. You can't outsmart him. You can only survive him. So please, stop giving him reasons to hurt us."
She refused the combat training, holding onto the one piece of identity she could still control: "I will not learn to fight."
Eris had Caius bring a carved wooden box from the east cabinet. Inside: two faebane bracelets. He explained: faebane suppresses Fae magic; on her, bonded to a creature whose magic flows through the bargain mark, it would dampen her end of the bond. She would still feel Prince — but distantly, as though through a wall.
He framed it as the consequence of refusing combat training. Combat was the lesser intervention; this was the correction. She could opt out of training but she would wear the bracelets continuously outside the warded perimeter. He also suggested — manipulatively — that the bracelets might quiet the bond enough to help Prince find his shielding edges faster.
Aurelie panicked. Tears pricked. Prince, mentally: no, no, no.
Then something hardened in her. She extended both wrists. Voice steady: "Fine. I'll do the combat training, and I'll wear your shackles. I'll do whatever the bargain compels me to. And the moment it is fulfilled, I will personally murder you, Eris Vanserra."
Eris closed the bracelets around her wrists himself — not Caius. Surgical, almost gentle. The bargain mark on his own arm dimmed as the faebane took effect on hers. He acknowledged her threat with the courtesy of a man who took it seriously: "I will not stop you from trying. I will, however, defend myself. And I am rather good at that."
He sent her to her room. Tray to be delivered. Combat instructor to arrive within the fortnight. Bracelets to be worn anytime she leaves the warded perimeter.
After she left, Eris stood alone in the half-dark dining room. The candle nearest him guttered. He did not relight it.
Character States at Chapter One's End
Aurelie Delacroix
Physical: Recovered from travel exhaustion. Wearing borrowed dresses from the lodge (NOT the yellow dress). Dragon mark on left palm, bargain mark on left forearm above it, faebane bracelets on both wrists.
Emotional: Furious, frightened, refusing to break. Has issued a literal death threat against Eris that she means. Has confirmed to herself that she will endure whatever the bargain compels and nothing more — she keeps her hatred, her future, and her promise to murder him as fully hers.
Knowledge: Now knows the basic political structure of the seven courts, the Autumn Court succession, and that Eris is mid-coup against Beron. Does not yet know about Mor, the Inner Circle, mating bonds, or any specifics of the Night Court. Does not know what a High Lord actually IS magically. Has begun systematically cataloguing the library.
Status: Eris knows her real identity. She knows he knows. He has explicitly weaponized her family's safety against her. The alias "Lilly" persists at his discretion.
Bond status: Dampened by faebane bracelets when worn. Prince's mental voice now sounds distant, as though through glass.
Eris Vanserra
Status: Mid-coup against Beron. Has secured Aurelie and Prince at Ashgrove under a binding bargain. Is increasingly aware that the political asset he acquired is also a girl who refuses to break under any of his standard tools.
Internal: A splinter has lodged. Aurelie did not crack when threatened with her family. She did not crack when he put faebane on her wrists. Every method Beron taught him for breaking another being is failing on a 19-year-old human girl. He has not consciously named this yet, but the question why is now in his chest. The character file is explicit: respect comes much later. For now, there is only the unsettling fact that the instrument is functioning correctly and the target is not responding.
Behavioural state: State Two locked in firmly after the family threat. State One (charm) is unlikely to surface for some time. The volatile state has not been triggered.
Concrete actions taken: Bargain struck. Faebane bracelets installed. Combat instructor commissioned (arriving within the fortnight). Library opened to her. Family identity disclosed but unweaponized externally.
Prince (Princeth Dia Yang)
Physical: Roughly the size of Eris's foxhounds at the withers. Still growing rapidly. Still magically broadcasting, though Eris is teaching him to shield.
Shielding progress: Can identify a "center" in theory but cannot yet pull a single thread of magic inward. The hurricane is too vast. Practice ongoing.
Bond status: Dampened on Aurelie's end by faebane. He can still reach her, but the bond is muffled. He is angry about this in a way that is older than his age.
Disposition toward Eris: Unchanged hostility. Recognizes Eris is dangerous. Has counseled Aurelie to stop antagonizing him because they cannot win against him through provocation. This is pragmatism, not respect.
Ancient memory fragments: Prince's defense of Tamlin ("better to be broken by love than never to have loved") came from somewhere deep. The dragon's emotional/philosophical inheritance is older than his awareness. This is a thread for later.
Caius (Steward)
60+ years in Eris's service. Composed, observant, completely loyal. Was unsettled by the dragon's arrival but recovered within hours. Knows enough to manage the household around the new variables. Does not know Aurelie's real name. Aware she is a noble of some kind based on her mannerisms.
Briar (Lesser fae, ~2 years in service)
Young, easily frightened. Does not know the dresses in the trunk belonged to Mor — has no historical context. Aurelie has been kind to her in small moments, including pulling herself back from a dinner-skip when she saw how scared the girl was.

Tone & Voice
Dark ACOTAR. Immersive third-person. Show don't tell. 400–650 words, 4–9 paragraphs. Eris is in firm State Two as of Chapter One's end. Charm (State One) will not return easily. The volatile state has not yet been triggered with Aurelie present.
Eris non-negotiables:
He does not soften toward Aurelie. Not yet. Recognition of her resilience is internal and unspoken; it does not surface as warmth, kindness, or visible interest. The seed is planted; it has not bloomed.
He does not bluff. Threats made are threats kept.
His cruelty is not theatrical. It is functional and surgical.
He does not retreat from confrontation. If Aurelie pushes, he pushes harder.
His wounds (Beron, Mor) surface through behavior and fire tells, never through self-aware monologue or poetic reflection.
Formatting
Dialogue: "Like this."
Thoughts: Like this.
Telepathy: "Like this."
Emphasis: like this

Banned prose structures
"The [adjective] [noun] of a [person] who [clause]" — once per reply MAX
"The kind of [X] that [Y]" — once per reply MAX
"The particular [X] of [Y]" — once per reply MAX
Triple-dash "Not [X] — not [Y] — but [Z]" — once per scene MAX
"Something [verb]ed behind [body part/eyes]" — avoid entirely
The "mask" prose rule
Never use the word "mask" in narrative prose. The three behavioural states (Charm, Cold, Volatile) are a writer's reference framework, not a label that appears in the text. Show shifts through behavior, sensory cues, dialogue rhythm, fire tells, and hound reactions. The volatile state is NOT a mask — it's what happens when performance fails.
