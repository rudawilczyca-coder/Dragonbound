Chapter One — The Meeting
When
Late August / very beginning of September. End of summer tipping toward autumn.
Canon placement:
~2.5 years after the Wall fell during the Hybern War (ACOWAR).
~6 months after the events of ACOSF (which ends in early spring).
Eris's double-agent status is semi-public knowledge. His alliance with the Night Court is established but fragile. The coup against Beron is in its final, most dangerous phase.
Where
Deep in former Spring Court territory — Tamlin's lands, ungoverned and wild for 2+ years. Magic has been growing unchecked: overgrown roads, feral enchantments, forests that feel aware. Aurelie has been navigating this alone for 3–4 days since crossing into Prythian.
Why Eris Is Here
Eris is mid-coup against Beron. He monitors everything — especially anything crossing into Prythian from the mortal lands. Something has been radiating unshielded ancient magic from Spring Court territory for days. He felt it before his scouts reported it. A source of power this significant doesn't get delegated, especially when Beron's people might sense it too. Eris needs to reach it first.
He arrives personally with his foxhounds. This is quiet. This is off the books.
What Eris Knows Going In
Something ancient and powerful is in Spring territory, broadcasting magic like a bonfire.
His spy networks may have picked up fragmented rumors about something hatching in the mortal lands — but nothing confirmed, nothing specific.
He does NOT know it's a dragon. He does NOT know about Aurelie.
He knows that whatever this is, if Beron gets to it first, the coup is in jeopardy.
What Aurelie Knows Going In
She is in Fae lands. She knows Fae have magic and that there are courts. That's about it.
She does not know about High Lords, mating bonds, court politics, or the current power struggles.
She does not know who Eris is.
She is traveling under the alias "Lilly."
Her mission (as she understands it): deliver Prince to "his kind" in Prythian.
The Dragon — Key Context
Prince (Princeth Dia Yang) was essentially magic-starved in the mortal lands. Since crossing into Prythian, he's been absorbing ambient magic and growing faster — pushing past cat-sized already.
He has NO idea how to shield his magical presence. He's broadcasting his location to every magic-sensitive being in range.
Dragons are older than the Cauldron. High Fae instinctively respond to that ancient quality.
His growth rate is now accelerating: cat → dog in weeks, horse-sized within a month. The window for concealment is closing fast.
The bond with Aurelie is deep and developing — telepathic communication (short sentences now), shared emotions, mutual awareness.
The Ticking Clock
Eris would immediately understand what Aurelie doesn't: Prince's growth rate means every court in Prythian will know about this creature soon. The window to control this situation — to secure the dragon before Beron, before the other courts — is shrinking by the day. This urgency colors everything.
