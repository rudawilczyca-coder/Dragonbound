## 1. Core Design Brief (What Ruby Wants)

1. **Deep ACOTAR Canon Roots (The 'Theo Nott' Principle):** Not a random stranger who falls out of a snowbank. He must have real history, real weight in Kallias and Viviane's court, and a reason to exist in the *ACOMAF*/*ACOWAR* timeline that feels like turning over a stone SJM placed on the map.
2. **Courts Aurelie Through Prince:** Where Julian saw Prince as a weapon to cage and Eris sees him as a political nuclear deterrent (and bacon thief), our Winter male recognizes Prince as an **endling and northern sky-kin**—courting Aurelie's trust by respecting her dragon as a sovereign being first.
3. **Heavyweight Counterweight to Eris Vanserra (MFM / Two-Men-One-Woman Viable):** Not Eris's blood brother (which keeps the threesome/MFM door wide open without sibling-weirdness), yet high enough in station, lethality, and presence that Eris cannot pull rank or intimidate him.

---

## 2. Three Canon-Rooted Origins in Kallias's Court

In *ACOMAF* and *ACOWAR*, SJM established three load-bearing facts about the Winter Court that give us our exact seam:
- **The Murder of the Two Dozen Children:** Under the Mountain, Amarantha slaughtered twenty-four Winter Court children. It left a generational frost across Kallias's territory—making anything *young, rare, and miraculous* (a newly hatched, last-of-his-kind ice-blue and white feathered dragon) almost sacred to Winter's old blood.
- **The Forty-Nine-Year Glacial Watch:** While Kallias was trapped UTM, Viviane and Kallias's fiercely loyal inner circle (commanders and scholars like **Ressina**) hid their people and held the northern mountain passes without a High Lord.
- **The Great Beasts of the North:** Winter is the only court whose war-host rides and fights alongside great white bears, snow-wolves, and high-altitude predators.

| Option | Title & Lineage | Canon Seam & Why He Travels With Us |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option A — The Bloodhound of the Glacier** | **Kallias's First Spear / Shield-Commander** (either **Viviane's older brother** or **Kallias's bastard half-brother** who held the northern passes for 49 years) | Second in authority only to Kallias and Viviane. He can go toe-to-toe with the heir of Autumn politically and militarily, never flinches at Eris's title, and is sent by Kallias as Winter's plenipotentiary envoy when word of a white-and-ice-blue dragon reaches the northern border. |
| **Option B — The Sky-Warden of the High Crags** | **Lord of the Glacial Eyrie / High Beast-Master of Winter** | Keeper of the northern beast-holds and the ancient bone-archives where dragons last nested in the high ice before Amarantha's age. He knows old dragon etiquette in his bones—how to bow without triggering a territorial strike, what raw marrow and frost-fish a growing drake needs, and why an *endling's* bond-mate cannot be broken like a court horse. |
| **Option C — The Combined Northern Lord (A + B)** | **Viviane's older brother (or Kallias's half-brother) & Sky-Warden of the Northern Peaks** | Combines both levers: blood-kin to the Winter throne *and* master of the high glacial beast-crags. Kallias sends him as Winter's envoy because he is the only male in Winter dangerous enough to ride alongside Eris Vanserra without getting eaten alive. |

## 4. Aesthetic, Build & Contrast with Eris

If **Eris Vanserra** is *smoke, crimson wool, sharp court masks, chess-board cruelty, rationed syllables, and hidden fire under the gloves*:

| Dimension | **Eris Vanserra (Autumn — Fire & Fox)** | **Our Winter Male (Winter — Ice & Timber Wolf)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Silhouette & Wardrobe** | Tailored court doublets, high collars, gloves that never come off in public, amber-gold/copper elegance | Floor-length black wool and charcoal fur-mantled greatcoat with silver-buckled leather straps in the snow (straight off Ruby's Pinterest feed) |
| **Physique** | Lean, lethal duelist's grace; built like a court fox | Taller and broader in the shoulders than Eris; heavy-muscled northern warrior built like a timber wolf |
| **Coloring** | Copper-red/auburn hair, amber-flame eyes | Wind-tousled ink-black hair with a streak of silver-white frost at the temple (or stark silver-white against windburned skin); **pale glacier-blue / ice-grey eyes that match Prince's scales** |
| **Voice & Register** | *The Collector & The Fox*: rationed syllables, double meanings, velvet barbs, an open Cost Ledger | **Silence and blunt weight**: doesn't play court games; watches Eris play them with a slow, unimpressed half-smile and says the one true thing nobody in the room was supposed to say out loud |
| **How He Treats Prince** | Sees a nuclear deterrent to burn Beron off a throne (and an infuriating hatchling who steals his bacon) | Drops to one knee in the snow on first meeting, hands open and clear of his twin blades, and addresses **Prince first** in the old northern form before asking Aurelie's name. Brings raw marrow-bones and frost-fish; offers the high open sky of the Winter peaks with zero strings |
| **Bedroom / MFM Dynamic** | Ruthless, un-gentle taker; sharp command, possessive heat, pins and claims (*Lucifer-coded edge*) | Grounded, massive, reverent-yet-unyielding northern strength—the immovable glacier wall against Eris's fire; holds her like spun glass or pins Eris's wrist when Eris gets too sharp |

  > *"Careful, Vanserra. Keep glaring at me like that and she'll start wondering whether you want to kill me or keep me warm."*
