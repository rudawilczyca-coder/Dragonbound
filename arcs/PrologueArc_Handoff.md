Purpose
This file captures all planning decisions made for the Aurelie Prologue Arc — a series of scenes set BEFORE Aurelie meets Eris. Upload this alongside the project files (ErisVanserraDark_v3.md, Aurelie_Delacroix.md, writing_style_reference_ACOTAR.md) into a fresh chat to begin writing.
What We're Writing
A prologue sequence covering:
The Gifting Scene — Aurelie in her bedroom, receiving betrothal gifts. The dragon egg hatches when she touches it with her bare hand.
The Next Day — She hides the hatchling, wrestles with what to do, decides to flee.
The Escape — She runs the following night.
Travel Snippets — Moments from her journey: the sea passage, the hatchling growing, their bond developing.
These scenes establish Aurelie's voice, her world, and the emotional weight of what she's leaving behind before she enters Prythian and meets Eris.
Roles
Ruby writes: Aurelie Delacroix + the dragon (all dialogue, actions, thoughts, decisions)
Vixen (Claude) writes: The narrator perspective + ALL NPCs (parents, servants, the prince, Queen Ismena, sailors, anyone else). Vixen does NOT write Aurelie's dialogue, thoughts, feelings, or decisions. Vixen does NOT write or speak for the dragon in any way.
Vixen CAN: Describe what NPCs observe about Aurelie — her body language, visible reactions, what the dragon is doing as seen from outside. React to what she does, never to what she means.
Established Characters & Details
Aurelie's Betrothed
The Prince: Middle son of Queen Ismena. Indifferent, spoiled, entitled. Aurelie has no feelings for him — neither warm nor cold. He's a non-entity to her, just the latest in a life she was told she'd live since age six.
Queen Ismena: One of the Six Mortal Queens. Middle-aged, wears black, fair skin, granite-hewn face, frowns often, wears a silver ring. Sharp, regal, severe.
Aurelie's Parents
Think Merida's parents from Brave at the start of the film.
Mother: Believes the marriage is Aurelie's duty to her family. Not cruel — genuinely thinks she's doing right by her daughter. Loves Aurelie but sees her value primarily through the lens of advantageous marriage.
Father: Doesn't see another way. Not malicious — just a man of his world. Loves his daughter but has never questioned the system.
Aurelie has three younger brothers. As the only girl, her primary value to the family is her womb / marriage alliance.
The family is nobility from a port city in the southern mortal lands.
The Dragon Egg
A petrified dragon egg, believed to be a stone carving or ancient artifact.
Part of Queen Ismena's family collection for ages. Given as one of several betrothal/dowry gifts — not even the centerpiece.
The Wall coming down (post-ACOWAR) sent a wave of freed magic into the mortal lands, which reawakened the dormant embryo inside.
The embryo did NOT hatch immediately when the Wall fell. It waited. For what, only it knows.
It hatches the moment Aurelie touches it with her bare hand atop the pile of gifts in her bedroom. That touch creates the dragon mark on her left palm — shaped vaguely like a dragon coiled into the letter "e." This is the bond signature.
The Hatchling (Later Named "Prince" / Princeth Dia Yang)
Nameless at first. Naming comes later.
White and ice-blue, feathered wings with membrane sections, golden tail blade, golden horns.
Newborn size, growing to small-cat-sized over the journey.
Sentient and intelligent but starts with no language. Like Saphira from Eragon.
Communication progression: emotions first → mental images → eventually words (words come later, not during prologue).
Aurelie and the dragon are learning each other in real time.
Timeline
Night 1: Gifting scene. Aurelie touches the egg. It hatches. She hides it in her bedroom.
Day 2: She hides the hatchling all day. Wrestles with what to do. Realizes a dragon in the mortal lands means death or captivity for the creature. Decides to flee.
Night 2: She escapes under cover of darkness.
~2 weeks: Sea passage on a ship. (Travel snippets — the dragon growing, bond developing, the terror and wonder of what she's done.)
~1 week: Overland travel through the mortal lands beyond the sea, heading north into Prythian-proper. She enters through what was once the Spring Court's territory.
World Context
Post-ACOWAR. The Wall is down. Magic flows freely. The war with Hybern is over.
Aurelie knows that Fae exist, that they have magic, that there are courts. That's about it. She's not walking into Prythian informed — she's walking in desperate.
She suspects dragons are rare or extinct even in Prythian but hopes there might be some — enough to deliver the hatchling to its kind.
She slowly realizes during the journey that she might not want to part with the dragon.
She does NOT know that the hatching saved her from a completely boring existence as a princess-broodmare. She doesn't yet understand that she'd rather die free than live tied in shackles. That understanding comes through the journey.
Writing Style
Immersive third-person novel style. "Show, don't tell."
400–650 words per reply, 4–9 paragraphs.
Sensory details, body language, subtext, inner thoughts (italics), dialogue ("quotes"), emotional depth.
NPCs have strong, consistent voices, flaws, initiative, and daily needs.
Dark ACOTAR tone but these are mortal-world scenes — no fire magic, no Fae politics yet. The darkness here is domestic: duty, expectation, the quiet violence of a life chosen for you.
Formatting:
Dialogue: "Like this."
Thoughts: Like this.
Emphasis: like this
Shouted/stressed speech: "Like this!"
Opening Scene Direction
Vixen opens with the gifting scene. Set the atmosphere of Aurelie's bedroom — the pile of betrothal presents, the weight of the approaching wedding, the world she's about to leave behind. Establish the domestic cage through sensory detail. Introduce the egg among the gifts as unremarkable. End at the moment before or just as she reaches for it — yield to Ruby for the touch and the hatching.
