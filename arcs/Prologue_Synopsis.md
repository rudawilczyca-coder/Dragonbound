What Happened
The Gifting Scene (Night 1)
Aurelie Delacroix, 19, daughter of a port-city noble family in the southern mortal lands, receives betrothal gifts from Queen Ismena's envoy. She is to marry Ismena's middle son — a prince she feels nothing for. Among dozens of lavish gifts sits an unassuming stone — oval, fist-sized, webbed with iridescent fractures. No one recognizes it for what it is.
That night, alone in her bedroom, Aurelie picks up the stone with her bare hand. It shatters in a blaze of blue-white light. A dragon hatchling emerges — white and ice-blue scales, feathered wings with membrane sections, golden horns, golden tail blade. The moment of contact sears a mark into Aurelie's left palm: a coiled dragon shape resembling the letter "e." The dragon bond is formed.
The hatchling is small (forearm-length), slick with luminous membrane, and impossibly ancient in its gaze. It is sentient and intelligent from birth but has no language yet. It recognizes Aurelie immediately and anchors to her touch.
The Decision (Day 2)
Aurelie hides the hatchling in a mahogany coffer. She navigates the household — her well-meaning but duty-bound mother (Lady Delacroix), her passive father (Lord Delacroix), her maidservant Lilly, and the farewell breakfast for Ismena's envoy (Aldric). She is distracted and her mother notices.
Key developments:
The dragon communicates hunger through the bond. Aurelie sneaks chicken livers from the kitchen at night; Cook (Gerda) blames the kitchen girl Mathilde for the missing food.
The dragon speaks its first word telepathically: "No" — refusing to be shut in the coffer again. The mental voice is vast, ancient, and overwhelming.
Lady Delacroix confronts Aurelie about her distraction and firmly shuts down Aurelie's attempt to postpone the wedding or travel.
Aurelie resolves to flee. She recruits Mathilde (kitchen girl, 16) to buy travel provisions — venison, cured meats, salt — paying her with a jewelry box. Mathilde uses a dockside butcher (Harren) to avoid detection.
Aurelie recruits her three younger brothers — Bastien (12, sharp and strategic), Emile (10, eager and reckless), Henri (8, beetle-obsessed) — to steal servants' clothes for her disguise. Payment: gifts from the betrothal pile (dagger and maps for Bastien, compass for Emile, an illuminated entomology manuscript for Henri).
Aurelie writes a farewell letter to Lilly, hidden with a box of sellable jewelry under a loose floorboard only the two of them know about. She apologizes for leaving and for pushing Lilly away.
Aurelie gives her name as "Lilly" when she later needs an alias.
The Escape (Night 2)
Aurelie leaves at 1:00 AM. The house is silent. She dodges the creaking third step, exits through the servants' entrance, and reaches the harbor — only to realize she forgot her coin purse on her dressing table. She cannot go back.
She spends the remaining hours before dawn huddled behind a warehouse crate near the docks, panicking.
Maren and the Tern's Wager
At dawn, Maren — first mate of the Tern's Wager, a three-masted cargo barque — spots Aurelie hiding. Maren is 43, sun-darkened, pragmatic, grey-braided, and has a reputation for taking on runaways as galley help. She offers passage in exchange for work: scrubbing pots, helping Cookie (Desmond), hauling water. No questions asked, no one touches her, whatever's in the bag stays her business.
Aurelie accepts. She boards at 11:00 AM, just before noon tide.
Captain Sorrel: Old, fair, drinks too much on Sundays.
Cookie (Desmond): Ship's cook, talks constantly, gossips and sings shanties.
Box: Bulky sailor who grunts.
Crew: ~11 total.
The Sea Voyage (~2 weeks)
Aurelie vomits for the first three days (leeward rail, as instructed).
Her hands blister from galley work. She does not complain.
Prince hides under her bunk by day. At night, she sneaks him on deck. He flies for the first time — terrifying her — and communicates amusement at her worry.
Aurelie proves she can read and count; Maren gives her the cargo manifest and upgrades her from hammock to bunk.
The Naming: On the second Sunday, Prince asks for a name. He has been silently cataloguing every person on the ship through Aurelie's senses. He rejects all legendary dragon names. He tells Aurelie: "You give me my name." Four days later, she names him Prince — after the human prince whose betrothal gift contained the egg. Full name: Princeth Dia Yang. His response: "Prince. I am Prince. And you are Mine."
Prince grows slowly but steadily. By journey's end he barely fits in the backpack.
Saltmarch and the Road North
The Tern's Wager docks at Saltmarch — a shrunken port town in the human lands south of former Wall territory. The town has adapted to two years of post-Wall reality: magical residue in the gutters, a weathered notice board of missing persons (all gone north, none returned), resigned locals who've stopped asking questions.
Maren's farewell: Pays Aurelie wages ("enough for provisions, not enough for anything foolish"). Parts with: "Don't drown."
Aurelie buys provisions from the Saltmarch butcher (60s, pragmatic, notices the bag moves). He provides critical intel:
3–4 days north on foot. Two villages: Dunhill (friendly, trades with travelers) and Cairnford (half-empty since last autumn).
Beyond Cairnford: Spring Court territory gone wild. Tamlin's lands are ungoverned; magic has been growing unchecked for 2+ years.
Fae riders patrol the north road — rotating banners, unknown allegiance (Spring remnants? Autumn scouts?). They turn back some travelers. Others simply disappear.
Advice: travel by day, no fires after dark past Dunhill, take the Wych Path (deer trail, eastern tree line) after Cairnford for canopy cover.
Free gift: salt for curing meat.
Crossing into Prythian (Day 4 of overland travel)
The road deteriorates as Aurelie walks north. Saplings with silvery bark grow unnaturally fast. The farmland goes feral. On the fourth day, the forest closes overhead and the air changes — sweeter, older, heavy with glittering pollen.
Aurelie finds a ruined waystation in a clearing carpeted with impossible flowers: copper-petaled, faintly glowing stems, chiming like finger-bells in the wind. The flowers turn toward her.
Prince goes silent — not absent, but reverent. His only words: "We are here."
Aurelie has crossed into Prythian. She stands in what was once Spring Court territory — Tamlin's wild, ungoverned lands.
Character States at Prologue's End
Aurelie Delacroix
Physical: Blistered hands, stronger than when she left, wearing stolen servants' clothes, carrying an overstuffed leather backpack and a smaller rucksack of provisions. Has a dagger (limited skill). Dragon mark on left palm (gold, coiled dragon/"e" shape).
Emotional: Determined, frightened, grieving what she left behind but committed. She believes her mission is to deliver Prince to "his kind" in Prythian. She has not yet consciously realized she doesn't want to part with him.
Knowledge: Knows Fae exist, have magic, and have courts. Knows almost nothing else about Prythian. Doesn't know about High Lords, mating bonds, or court politics. She is walking in blind.
Alias: Traveling as "Lilly."
Prince (Princeth Dia Yang)
Physical: Cat-sized, barely fits in the backpack. White/ice-blue scales, feathered wings with membrane, golden horns, golden tail blade (opens/closes). Growing steadily.
Communication: Telepathic. Started with emotions/images, progressed to single words, now speaks in short sentences. Voice is ancient, vast, sometimes overwhelming. Has a dry wit and a possessive streak.
Bond: Deep and developing. They sense each other's presence, emotions, and hunger. He can see through her eyes. She can feel his moods. The bond mark on her palm pulses gold occasionally.
Behavior: Insatiably curious, fiercely intelligent, protective. His golden tail blade locks open when alert or threatened. He went reverent and silent upon entering Prythian.
Key quote: "Prince. I am Prince. And you are Mine."

Unresolved Threads
The Fae riders on the north road — whose orders? Spring remnants? Autumn scouts? Aurelie has not yet encountered them.
Aurelie's family — the household has discovered her absence. What actions have they taken? Has word reached Queen Ismena?
Lilly's letter — still hidden under the floorboard. Lilly hasn't found it yet.
Aurelie's alias — she is traveling as "Lilly." This will matter when she meets Fae who can smell lies.
Prince's origins — the egg was in Ismena's collection for generations. Where did it come from? Why did it wait for Aurelie specifically?
Prince's growth rate — accelerating. He will soon be too large to hide.
The dragon bond — still developing. Full extent of abilities/consequences unknown.
Aurelie's self-knowledge — she believes she's on a delivery mission. She hasn't realized the dragon saved her, or that she'd rather die free than live in shackles. That understanding is still forming.
