Week 3 (Days 16–22): Type 3 — The open-door interrogation. Cruelty performed near her, not on her. Understanding permanently altered.
Week 4 (Days 23–30): Type 2 — The volatile state. Loss of control. The throat-grab. Prince's intervention. The Instrument breaks. Everything shifts.

📖 CHAPTER 2 — The Settling
WEEK 1: Establishing the Rhythm (Sept 15–21)
🌾 Day 1 (Sept 15) — INHABIT FULLY
Already in progress in the Chapter 2 chat.
Combat: Second training session of the day with Caelum. Eris arrives unannounced and watches the full session without speaking. Caelum's ears do one thing and recover.
Faebane: Established — Aurelie wore the bracelets to the south courtyard for training. The ritual of Caius locking them on at the perimeter, unlocking them when she returns inside. Twice daily. She doesn't comment on it anymore. The silence around the ritual is its own kind of submission.
Prince: Watches from the balcony. Too large to perch on her shoulder now. His commentary on Eris's presence is dry and telepathic — Aurelie has to control her face.
Dinner: Eris dines with her. Cold register. He asks pointed questions about what Caelum taught her. She gives minimal answers. The evening ends early.
🌾 Day 2 (Sept 16) — INHABIT
Stables (morning): First visit. Aurelie wanders in before training and notices Brille — the auburn mare with the white star that nobody rides. Tomas redirects her toward a calmer horse. She doesn't push. She just looks at the mare for a few minutes and leaves. Tomas files this.
Combat: Caelum teaches falls. Aurelie hits the ground for the first time and discovers how much it hurts. She gets up seven times. Caelum nods once.
Breaking attempt: Eris has her dinner sent to her room on a tray — no invitation to the dining table. The message: access to him is a privilege, not a right. She eats on her balcony with Prince and finds she prefers it. This is not the response he wanted.
🌾 Day 3 (Sept 17) — SKIM
Pattern day. Training (falls, continued). Library reading — Prythian political geography. Tray dinner again, but this time by her choice — she sends word through Briar that she'll eat in her room. Eris notes that she has reframed his punishment as her preference. The power dynamic of the tray is now muddied. He is irritated.
Single image: Prince watching her wince every time she sits, dry telepathic commentary about humans being constructed from wet paper.
🌾 Day 4 (Sept 18) — INHABIT
Stables (morning): Second visit. She reads aloud to Brille — a chapter of a Prythian history textbook, because she has nothing else. Tomas is in the next stall, pretending not to hear. The mare lets her stay. She is late for training because of this.
Caelum — Landmark 1: The first time he makes her laugh. Real laughter, not polite. Something dry about Eris that's technically deniable but the implication is clear. She laughs. Her own laughter startles her — she hasn't laughed in months. His ear angles forward. He pretends not to notice it landed.
Dinner — first proper dinner in days: She attends. She is civil. Eris notices the civility and doesn't trust it. Body plot ticks: her hair is coming loose at the temples from training. He catalogues the loose hair and hates that he catalogued it.
Breaking attempt (subtle): Over dinner, Eris mentions — casually, as if reading from a report — that Mathilde has been promoted to assistant cook at the Delacroix household. The information he should not have. Aurelie's hand tightens on her fork. She continues eating. She asks no questions. He now knows: she understood the message. He has eyes on Hightower. Her family is visible to him.
🌾 Day 5 (Sept 19) — SKIM
Routine. Training. Library. Eris dines in his study; Aurelie eats alone in the dining room with a book.
Household shift (small): Caius brings the soup himself instead of sending Briar. Aurelie notices. She thanks him by name. He nods — no warmth, but no coldness either. This is Caius softening in increments so small only she would notice.
🌾 Day 6 (Sept 20) — INHABIT
Stables (morning): Brille eats an apple from Aurelie's hand for the first time. Tomas almost smiles. Almost.
Prince's shielding: Eris runs the lesson directly. First failure mode discovered: Prince can briefly compress his signature but the moment he's startled — by a laugh, a foxhound passing — the shield collapses entirely. Eris is frustrated. Prince is exhausted. Aurelie is told to leave the courtyard. She obeys. The obedience costs her visibly.
Breaking attempt: At dinner, Eris tells her that she will not attend Prince's shielding sessions going forward. Her presence is a distraction to the dragon's focus. This is strategically defensible and emotionally cruel — he's separating her from the only thing she came to Prythian for. She argues. He does not budge. Cold register, absolute.
Aurelie's response: She eats three bites and excuses herself. He lets her go without comment. The fact that he lets her go bothers him later. He expected her to fight harder. The lack of fight is diagnostic data he can't quite read.
🌾 Day 7 (Sept 21) — INHABIT, BIG
THE WALL PIN.
Morning: Combat training ran long. Caelum stayed an extra fifteen minutes correcting her knife grip. Eris arrived at the end of the session and saw the extra time. Filed it.
Evening: Dinner. Caelum is present in the hall — eating with Caius at a side table, technically as Caius's guest. Aurelie makes a comment about Eris's ability to keep his own household loyal. The comment is aimed at Eris but witnessed by Caelum. Caelum's face does one thing and recovers.
The corridor, after dinner: Eris pulls her aside. Pins her against the wall. His hand beside her head, not on her — the proximity doing the work. Heat radiating from his palm into the stone. The charming register has dropped entirely. Cold. Direct.
She doesn't flinch. She looks at his hand against the stone, then back at his face. Calm. Observant. And says:
"You smell like smoke when you're angry."
Beat.
"Go on, then."
He releases her. Walks away. Burns down half a cord of wood in his study that night.
The next morning: Caelum arrives for training carrying two waterskins for the first time. He saw. He is adjusting. He never says a word about it.
WEEK 2: The First Escalation (Sept 22–29)
🌾 Day 8 (Sept 22) — SKIM
Eris in a foul mood all day. Working on cipher correspondence with Az — his focus is fractured since the wall pin. He skips dinner.
Household shift: Aurelie eats with Briar in the kitchen at Briar's tentative invitation. First time she's eaten with a servant since arriving. It would horrify her noble mother. She finds she doesn't care. Briar is less frightened around her than around anyone else in the lodge. Aurelie notices this and holds it carefully.
🌾 Day 9 (Sept 23) — INHABIT
Stables (morning): Aurelie touches Brille's neck for the first time without the mare flinching. Tomas hands her a brush wordlessly. She brushes the mare's coat for twenty minutes. Late for training.
Combat: Caelum starts teaching her how to break a hold from behind. His hands adjust her shoulders. The physical contact is professional and significant simultaneously. His ears are doing things. She's watching his ears. Neither comments.
Breaking attempt — THE PLATE: Aurelie skipped a practice protocol meeting Eris had scheduled that morning (she was at the stables and lost track of time — genuinely, not defiance, but Eris reads it as defiance). Her dinner is sent to his study. When she comes looking for it, he's eating. Her plate is on the floor. He doesn't say anything. He just eats. The silence is the instruction.
Aurelie's response: She takes the plate. She sits on the floor. She eats with perfect noble posture, cross-legged on his study floor, and looks him in the eyes the entire time. She turns his humiliation into her performance. She makes kneeling look like a queen granting audience.
He is more destabilized by her compliance than he would have been by her refusal. His fork pauses for a half-second. She sees the pause.

🌾 Day 11 (Sept 25) — INHABIT

Body plot: She watches him cross the courtyard from the library window. Notes how he carries his shoulders when he thinks no one is watching. The exhaustion changes the shape of him. She files this and does not examine why she filed it.

Caelum — Landmark 2: The elbow-catch. Walking back from the courtyard, she stumbles over a tree root — exhausted from a brutal session. He catches her elbow, steadies her, releases. Two seconds. She thinks about it the rest of the day. He pretends he's forgotten. He hasn't.

Recovery day. Aurelie didn't realize how much the dinner performance would cost her emotionally. Light training. Long library afternoon.
Eris debriefs with Caius about Maelis's reaction. Maelis is interested in "the human girl with the creature." The coup machinery has a new variable to exploit.
🌾 Day 14 (Sept 28) — INHABIT
Stables (morning): Tomas mentions, in passing, that his daughter is unwell — chest cold from working in the kitchens of a neighboring estate. Aurelie asks her name. He hesitates. He gives it: Una. She files it. He gave her his daughter's name. This is a marker.

Dinner: Eris is watching her differently. She catches him watching twice. She doesn't comment. He doesn't explain. Body plot synchronizing — they're now both aware they're noticing each other, and both aware the other knows.
Breaking attempt — information starvation: Aurelie asks about dragon lore — has his library or his contacts in the Inner Circle turned up anything? Eris says he'll look into it. He will not look into it. He is withholding the thing she wants most (information about Prince's kind) as a control mechanism. She cannot prove he's stalling. She suspects.
🌾 Day 15 (Sept 29) — INHABIT, CHAPTER 2 CLOSER
The Library Watching Scene.
Late evening. Aurelie reading by candlelight — she's found a treatise on Autumn Court succession laws and is taking notes in cipher.
Eris stands in the doorway watching for a full minute before she notices him. The whole minute. He is not in any register. He is just a man watching a girl read a book in his library.
When she looks up, he's already reset to State Two. But she saw the reset happen. He sees her see it.
Brief exchange. Cordial. He asks what she's reading. She tells him: Autumn Court succession laws. He doesn't react visibly. He says goodnight. He walks the length of the east wing hallway slowly enough that she hears every step.
She does not sleep well. He does not sleep at all.
Chapter 2 closes.
📖 CHAPTER 3 — The Tightening
WEEK 3: The Understanding Shifts (Sept 30 – Oct 6)

First time he names the Inner Circle to her. She processes. She agrees because she has no choice. He notes she did not negotiate. Interesting data.
Combat: Caelum runs a brutal session because she asked him to. (The political stakes are getting concrete — she wants to be functional faster.) He pushes her hard. She doesn't break. His professional admiration leaks through the frame.
Household punishment (second instance): Briar disappears for two days starting this evening. No explanation given. Aurelie asks Caius where Briar went. Stables: Aurelie rides Brille around the inner courtyard for the first time. Not far — just a slow walk in a circle while Tomas holds the bridle. He releases it. She rides one full loop on her own. First thing she has accomplished at Ashgrove that is entirely her own. She tells no one.

🍂 Day 18 (Oct 2) — INHABIT
Briar returns. Paler than before. Will not make eye contact with Aurelie for two days. Aurelie understands: I did this to her by being kind to her. The realization is a knife.
THE LUCIEN LETTER. Mid-afternoon. Courier arrives — Inner Circle livery, hand-delivered. Eris reads it in the entrance hall and burns it in front of the courier without responding. Aurelie sees from the staircase.
After the courier leaves, she asks Caius. He hesitates, then: "The heir's brother. He has more than one, of course. But the one in the Night Court is who Lord Vanserra burns letters from."
Aurelie now knows there is a Lucien Vanserra in the Inner Circle. And Caius gave her that information voluntarily. The household is shifting under Eris. She banks this intel.
🍂 Day 19 (Oct 3) — INHABIT, GUT-PUNCH
THE LIBRARY DISCOVERY.
Morning: routine training. Afternoon: Aurelie is in the library. She's been working through the east shelves systematically — political histories, court records, administrative documents. Today she pulls a bound ledger of Autumn Court border settlement taxation records from approximately eighty years ago.
She reads it. She was trained in estate management as a noble daughter — she understands ledgers. The numbers tell a story: taxation rates on lesser fae border settlements running at 70-80% of yield. Penalty levies for "insufficient production." Forced labor conscription quotas. A column for "settlement dissolution" — entire communities dissolved and their populations redistributed when they failed to meet targets.
She cross-references with what Tomas told her about Una's mother — dead, from a settlement that "was reorganized" when Una was three. The euphemism clicks into place. Reorganized means dissolved. Tomas's wife died during a forced relocation.
Aurelie is physically ill. She puts the ledger down. She sits in the library for an hour without moving. Prince, through the bond, feels her nausea and her rage. He says nothing. He doesn't need to.
Evening: she goes to dinner. She is perfectly composed. Eris notices nothing different. She has decided something, and she has not told him what.
🍂 Day 20 (Oct 4) — SKIM
Aurelie writes her longest journal entry yet. Six pages of cipher. She is processing the ledger. She is also processing that the system that made her a broodmare in the mortal lands is the same system that dissolved Tomas's settlement and killed his wife.
Light training. Caelum notices she's distracted but doesn't push. His ears angle slightly back — he's heard something in her silence he can't identify.
Eris is in his study all day. Light under his door until 4am.
🍂 Day 21 (Oct 5) — INHABIT
TYPE 3 CRUELTY — THE OPEN DOOR.
A prisoner arrives at Ashgrove mid-morning. Someone who betrayed Eris's network — a real traitor, a lesser fae who sold information about troop movements to one of Beron's loyalist houses. Real consequences. Real stakes.
Eris conducts the interrogation in the entrance hall. He does not invite Aurelie. He does not close the door.
She hears it from the staircase. The sounds. Questions asked in that warm, charming voice. The silences between answers that stretch too long. The smell of burning that drifts upstairs.
She sits in her room with her hands over Prince's ears. Prince can hear through the bond regardless. The sounds stop after an hour. Eris walks past her door afterward. His footsteps are even. He smells of smoke and nothing else — he has already washed his hands.
This changes her understanding permanently. The bath is hot because this man keeps the fires burning. She sleeps safely because someone else does not. She knew this intellectually. Now she knows it in her body.
But — and this is the crucial turn — the ledger from Day 19 is also in her mind. Beron's settlements. The forced relocations. The 70% taxation. What Eris did today is brutal. What Beron does every day, systematically, to thousands is worse. She is not excusing Eris. She is weighing.
🍂 Day 22 (Oct 6) — INHABIT
Caelum — Landmark 3. Aurelie arrived at training visibly unwell — the open door, the sounds, a night of no sleep. She tries to push through. Caelum calls the session early.
"You don't have to prove anything to me. I'm not him."
Immediate self-correction: "That came out wrong. The work is the work, and the work today is rest."
She sees the deflection happen in real time. She doesn't push. Caelum has shown his hand. He sees what's happening and it costs him.
Practice dinner #3 — Lord Aedric. Beron loyalist. Eris invited him to test the loyalty signal. Aurelie performs perfectly. Aedric makes a comment about her being human. Eris does not intervene. She handles it herself — a single line that redirects the room's attention without raising her voice.
Aurelie's shift (internal): She's no longer just performing at practice dinners. Since the ledger, she's gathering intelligence. She watches Aedric's tells. She notes which topics make him uncomfortable. She files his weak points. She is not yet sharing this with Eris. She's building her own map.
WEEK 4: The Breaking and the Becoming (Oct 7 – Oct 15)
🍂 Day 23 (Oct 7) — SKIM
Aurelie's journal entry: three pages processing Aedric. She has begun writing tactical observations alongside personal entries. The journal is becoming a political document.
She returns to the library. Pulls more ledgers. Finds records of Beron's treatment of fox-touched communities specifically — the communal settlements on the southeastern border. Higher taxation. Fewer protections. Treated as disposable labor pools. She thinks of Caelum, who grew up in one of these settlements. She does not mention this to him.
🍂 Day 24 (Oct 8) — INHABIT
Stables: She rides Brille beyond the courtyard for the first time — into the cleared ground south of the lodge, at dawn, when the wards are visible. She rides through the shimmer. The mare is steady. Something shifts inside her.
Tomas watches from the stable door. Nods once.
Prince's shielding — THE BREAKTHROUGH. He holds for ninety seconds. First sustained achievement. Eris's pride is visible — a half-smile he forgets to suppress. Aurelie is not present but Prince reports through the bond. Prince's commentary: "Do not get used to that. He will compensate within the hour."

Breaking attempt — Caelum weaponized. At dinner, Eris mentions — almost in passing — that the training contract has a review clause. He's pleased with Aurelie's progress, of course. But contracts are contracts. He'll evaluate whether the arrangement is still necessary at the end of the month. The threat is clear: Caelum's presence is not guaranteed. Aurelie feels the floor shift. She keeps eating. She does not look at Eris. She does not give him the satisfaction.
🍂 Day 25 (Oct 9) — INHABIT
THE DAWN BALCONY.
Aurelie wakes early and steps onto her balcony to watch the wards shimmer. Eris is at his bedroom window. They see each other. The wards shimmer between them. The distance of the east wing hallway measured in architecture. Neither moves. Neither waves. They look at each other for a long beat in the predawn light.
She steps back inside first.
He is still there when the wards fade.
🍂 Day 26 (Oct 10) — INHABIT
Eris leaves Ashgrove — two-day trip. Beron's nameday celebration at the Forest House. He is required to attend. He goes.
Aurelie has Ashgrove to herself. She spends the afternoon in the stables. Evening: she eats with Tomas. Bread and cheese and wine from his own bottle. He tells her about Una for ten minutes. She listens — doesn't perform listening, just listens. He hasn't had anyone listen to him in a long time.
She asks, carefully: "What happened to Una's mother?" He tells her. Settlement reorganized. His wife didn't survive the relocation march. Una was three. He does not elaborate. He does not need to. Aurelie has now connected the ledger to a face, a name, a child she hasn't met. The political is fully personal.
🍂 Day 27 (Oct 11) — INHABIT
Eris still gone. Aurelie spends the entire day in the unmarked library room. Copies more crests, more passages. Finds a hand-bound volume in a script she partially recognizes — old fae, similar to mortal Latin. She can pick out one word in fifteen. Files the volume for later.
She writes a journal entry that is entirely political analysis. No personal content. She is mapping Autumn Court power structures from the library's records, cross-referencing with what she's learned at practice dinners. She is building an intelligence picture that Eris does not know she has.
🍂 Day 28 (Oct 12) — INHABIT
Eris returns from the Forest House at midday. He looks carved out. Whatever happened at Beron's nameday cost him. He goes straight to his study. Six hours. Door closed.
Aurelie does not approach. She wants to and the wanting alarms her.
Dinner: Eris in State One at maximum charm. The performance is so polished she can see the seams. She doesn't comment. She lets him perform. He notices her not commenting. He can't tell if she's being merciful or letting him hang himself.
Caelum, dining in the hall at Caius's invitation, watches the exchange. His ears do one thing and recover. All three of them just exchanged information without speaking.
Practice dinner #4 — Lord Maelis returns with his wife, Lady Maelis. The wife is sharp — asks Aurelie probing questions about Hightower, the Mortal Queens, her mother's family. Aurelie answers every question without lying (the bargain prevents it) but answers in ways that give nothing useful. Eris is genuinely impressed by the technique. He won't say so yet.
🍂 Day 29 (Oct 13) — INHABIT, DEVASTATING
TYPE 2 CRUELTY — THE VOLATILE STATE.
The fight builds all day. Aurelie has spent the morning re-reading the bargain terms in her mind (the magic transcribed them — she carries the exact wording). She realizes: there is nothing in the bargain requiring her active assistance. She is bound to stay, bound not to contact other courts independently, bound not to lie to him directly. But she is not bound to help. The gap is real. The gap is leverage.
At dinner, she deploys it. She tells him she's been thinking about the bargain. She notes, clinically, that she could walk into Day Court and say twelve words and end his coup. She's not threatening it. She's proving she's thought about it. She's showing him she has leverage he didn't close.
Eris goes cold. State Two. Surgical. He tells her what would happen to her family.
She says:
"You keep threatening my family like it's a trump card. But you haven't touched them in two months. You won't. Because the moment you hurt them, you lose the only leverage that works on me, and then you have nothing. You're not your father, Eris. You just wish you were, because then at least you'd be something instead of a man playing dress-up in his daddy's cruelty."
The volatile state detonates. His hand is around her throat and she's against the wall and the stone behind her is heating up and his eyes have gone full gold. No register. No calculation. Five centuries of damage expressing itself through his hands.
The heat at her back builds toward actually dangerous. His grip is real. Her feet are off the ground. He is shaking — not with rage, with the effort of the volatile state trying to become something worse.
Prince intervenes. Not a roar. Not an attack. A single telepathic image sent directly into Eris's mind — something Prince has never done before. The image: Eris as a child, pinned against a wall by Beron's hand in exactly the same position. A mirror. He is his father. He is doing what was done to him.
He drops her.
She hits the ground. Red marks on her throat. Heat-reddened skin on her back. Nothing permanent. But the line has been crossed. He physically hurt her. Not as strategy. As loss of control.
He leaves the room. Locks himself in his study. The foxhounds outside the door whine — they have never been afraid of him before.
Prince's voice to Aurelie, very quiet: "Are you hurt?" She says she's fine. She is lying. He knows. He says nothing else. The bond pulses gold.
🍂 Day 30 (Oct 14) — INHABIT
THE PRIVATE BREAKING — and the getting up.
Morning: Caelum arrives for training. Sees the marks on her throat. His ears flatten and stay flat for the entire session. He does not ask. She does not explain. The session is the quietest one they've ever had. He calls an early break. She drinks from the second waterskin. At the end he says only: "Same time tomorrow." It is the only thing he can offer.
Afternoon: Aurelie locks herself in the green room. Prince curls around her. She sobs into his neck for an hour. The bond burns gold. The cumulative weight — the bracelets, the plate, the open door, the household punishments, Briar's pale face, the Lilly intelligence, the bargain gap, and now his hand on her throat — all of it lands at once. She breaks. Privately. Completely.
She gets up. Washes her face. Pins her hair. Puts on the dress she wore to Lady Vere's dinner. Goes downstairs.
Evening — THE LIBRARY. She goes to return a book. Eris is already there. Working. Documents spread. He looks up when she enters. He does not perform State One. He does not perform anything.
Neither does she.
She returns the book. Picks another. Sits down at the other end of the same table.
They work in silence for two hours. He on coup correspondence. She on Prythian history. Neither speaks. Neither leaves.
The silence is not comfortable. It is not hostile. It is the silence of two exhausted people who have spent a month trying to destroy each other and have just realized they are both still standing.
Caelum, walking the corridor to his quarters, passes the library doorway and sees them. Sees the silence. Sees what kind of silence it is. His ears flatten and stay flat for the rest of the walk to his room.
He does not sleep.
Aurelie eventually rises. Says goodnight. Eris says goodnight. He does not watch her leave. The choice not to watch is its own confession.
🍂 Day 31 (Oct 15) — TRANSITION TO CHAPTER 4
Morning. Pattern resumes on the surface. Caelum at dawn. Training. Library. Dinner.
Underneath: everything has shifted.
Eris has not mentioned the throat-grab. He has not apologized (he does not apologize). He has not acknowledged it happened. But his behavior has changed — he is no longer trying to break her. He does not know what he is doing instead. The instrument broke on Day 29 and he has not yet built a replacement.
Aurelie has not mentioned the throat-grab either. She has filed it as data. The data: the cruelty is not all of him. There is something underneath that stopped. The stopping is the important information.
She has also, sometime between the green room sobbing and the library silence, made a decision she has not announced. She is going to help him take the throne. Not for him. For Tomas. For Una. For Briar. For every lesser fae under Beron's boot. She will tell him when she's ready. She is not ready yet. She is going to make him earn it.
End-of-Arc Character States
