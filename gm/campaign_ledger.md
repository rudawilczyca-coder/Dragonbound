## 1. The Lucien Visit & His Unexpected Companion

### Who Comes With Lucien?
When Eris invites Lucien to Ashgrove to test Aurelie (and bait the Night Court's curiosity without triggering an open border incident), Lucien does **not** ride into the Autumn woods alone.
- **Candidate A (The Vassa / Jurian Wildcard — Band of Exiles):** **Lord Jurian** (or **Queen Vassa**, the cursed firebird queen in human form by day).
  - *Why Vassa is electric:* Vassa is a cursed human queen bound to a mythical bird of fire by Koschei's magic; Aurelie is a human noblewoman bound by a bargain mark to the Heir of Autumn and bonded to an ancient dragon. Vassa is sharp-tongued, aristocratic, takes zero nonsense from Fae lords, and immediately clocks that Aurelie is *not* a simple maidservant named "Lilly"—while Eris and Vassa despise/respect each other on sight like two apex birds circling the same carrion.
  - *Why Jurian is electric:* Jurian is an ancient mortal general who survived five hundred years as Amarantha's eye-in-a-ring, knows every dirty secret of High Fae history (including the old legends from before the Treaty when dragons vanished), and delights in getting under Eris's skin at the dinner table while casually dropping a cryptic historical clue about dragons and the Winter Mountains.
- **Candidate B (The Night Court Shadow):** **Azriel** (waiting unseen in the trees beyond the one-mile ward line, testing the perimeter where Prince's ancient magic hums against Eris's wards) OR **Mor** refusing to step foot in Autumn while sending a shadow-watched envoy.
- *GM Play Plan:* Have Lucien arrive at Ashgrove with **Jurian** (or **Vassa**—depending on which dynamic Ruby bites hardest on when the horses pull into the courtyard!). Better yet: Lucien brings **Jurian** inside the gates as his "human-realm liaison" from the manor on the continent/Velaris fringe, because Lucien heard a rumor that Eris acquired a *mortal girl from across the sea* and brought the one mortal in his circle who knows the eastern kingdoms' heraldry!
  - *The Live-Fire Trap:* Jurian takes one look at Aurelie's posture, her hands, and her phrasing, and realizes she is highborn Continental nobility (*Hightower*), forcing Aurelie and Eris into a razor-wire dance at the dinner table to keep her family's name out of Lucien's and Jurian's mouths!

## 2. The "Show Dinner" (Autumn Court Associates & Vultures)

### The True Purpose of the Dinner
Eris hosts four high-ranking Autumn nobles at Ashgrove under the guise of an autumn hunting supper:
1. **Lord Vastor of the Copper Marches** — Old-blood warlord who controls the eastern garrison roads; cynical, half-convinced Beron is past his prime, testing whether Eris has the stomach to kill his father or if Eris has gone soft in his country lodge.
2. **Lady Meridia of Redmount** — Silk-voiced poisoner and master of whispers in the eastern boroughs; openly allied with Eris's faction, **secretly taking gold from Beron's second son (Kallum)** to find out what the "white shadow" spotted over Ashgrove's ridge actually is.
3. **Lord Cassander** — Arrogant lesser lord who despises mortals and immediately treats "Lilly" the cupbearer as a toy to be humiliated—until Aurelie's razor-sharp "accidental" spill or barbed courtesy turns the table's laughter onto him, and later that night Eris deals with Cassander's insolence in private with lethal Autumn coldness.

### The Secret Hook at the Show Dinner
- While standing behind Eris's chair pouring wine, Aurelie will overhear a coded exchange between Lady Meridia and Lord Vastor about **a sealed iron vault under the Forest House**—and a shipment of Continental glass and chains that arrived from **Queen Ismena's ships** three months before Aurelie ever fell through the portal!
- *The Reveal Seed:* Queen Ismena of Hightower didn't find Dragon Egg #312 by accident. Someone in Prythian (or Koschei across the water, trading through Autumn intermediaries) knew what that petrified stone was before Aurelie ever touched it.

## 3. What Each High Lord Actually Wants (The Court Tour Matrix)

### I. Summer Court — High Lord Tarquin (Adriata)
- **What Eris tells Aurelie:** We are going to trade eastern timber tariffs for coastal shipping rights and leak a rumor to Helion's scholars.
- **What Tarquin actually wants (Hidden Hook):** Something has been hunting ships in the deep trenches south of Adriata—not sea wyrms, and not Hybern remnants, but cold currents radiating from the continental shelf. Tarquin's water-seers sense Prince's ancient pre-Treaty magic the moment they enter Adriata's palace and mistake Prince for an omen tied to the **Drowned Archive** beneath the Summer palace.
- **The Twist:** Tarquin offers Aurelie genuine warmth and sanctuary—not out of pity like Lucien, but because he believes a bonded dragon-rider shouldn't be leashed to Beron's heir. Yet Tarquin's cousin **Cresseida** quietly tries to bargain for a single feather or scale of Prince's shed plumage to power Summer's wards.

### II. Day Court — High Lord Helion Spellcleaver
- **What Helion actually wants (Hidden Hook):** Helion knows Eris and Lucien's deepest family secret (that **Lucien is Helion's biological son** by Lady Vanserra, and that Eris has protected his mother's secret for centuries at the cost of his own reputation).
- **The Dragon Codex Vault:** When Helion opens the pre-Treaty dragon vaults for Aurelie and Prince, the oldest codex is written in a dialect of the Old Language that Fae magic cannot untangle—only **Prince's breath or Aurelie's blood-bonded touch** can wake the ink.
- **What the Codex Reveals:** Dragons did not go extinct in Prythian by accident. At the end of the ancient wars, the last great flight of white-and-gold dragons retreated north into the **deepest glacial caverns of the Winter Court** and sealed themselves into stone sleep—waiting for a bloodline that could wake them.

### III. Winter Court — High Lord Kallias & Lady Viviane
- **Prince's True Nature & The Kallias Bond (Locked Canon):** Prince's true elemental nature is **ICE, not fire**—which is why he is drawn to the Winter Court from the beginning! In Winter, **Prince speaks to High Lord Kallias mind-to-mind** (the *only* High Lord he ever grants telepathic speech to, just like Aurelie), and **Kallias offers Prince a permanent nesting place in the Winter Court** whenever he wishes it.
- **What This Does to Eris:** Eris bargained for a dragon assuming he had acquired an apex weapon of fire for Autumn's throne—only to watch the ancient creature of pure ice ignore every other lord in Prythian, speak mind-to-mind with Autumn's elemental rival Kallias, and receive an open invitation to make Winter his permanent home (and take Aurelie with him once the bargain ends!).
- **What Kallias actually wants (Hidden Hook):** Kallias and Viviane fiercely distrust the Autumn Court (especially after the war and the border tensions). Worse, recent seismic tremors in the high glaciers—triggered from afar on the exact day Prince hatched and crossed into Prythian—have cracked the ice above the ancient Frost-Cradle dragon barrows. Kallias initially suspects Eris of trespassing on Winter's oldest sacred mystery, until Prince speaks directly into Kallias's mind and proves his sovereign ice nature.
- **The Pressure Cooker:** In the freezing crystal halls of the Winter Court, Eris's fire magic makes him an immediate pariah—and for the first time, **Eris has to rely on Aurelie and Prince's ice-bright affinity** to keep them from being thrown into Kallias's dungeons, while sharing a single hearth-warmed suite in a palace of ice.

## 5. Night 1 in the Hidden Hollow — The Midnight Complication ("Dealer's Choice", Prepped 18 Nov 2026)
*(Triggered by Ruby's 18 Nov 19:48 ask: "I want something to happen at night, dealer's choice. But I don't want the road to go too smoothly 😏🗡️")*

### A. The Setup & Double Detonator
- **Why tonight goes sideways:** In Turn 3, Eris revealed he sent a morning dispatch pulling the southern game-wardens off the royal red-stag preserve four miles north until dawn so Prince could hunt undisturbed. What Eris doesn't know yet is that **someone inside the Forest House (Kallum's network via Lady Meridia's runners) flagged that counter-order within the hour**.
- **The Hunter Pack:** **Vane of the Copper Fen** (one of Beron's ruthless kennel-captains from the Forest House) and four mounted blood-trackers with **two ash-collared bog-hounds** rode hard down the military trace to find out *why* Eris cleared a four-mile royal valley on the eve of a border crossing.
- **Beat 1 — The Bond Snap & The Ward Chime (Interrupting the Fireside Scene):**
  - Right as the fireside tension between Eris (kneeling between Aurelie's boots, warming her thighs) and Aurelie hits a peak on Turn 5/6, **two alarms fire at once**:
    1. **Aurelie's bond with Prince snaps cold-alert four miles north** (riders with iron-tipped repeater crossbows and flare-horns just entered the lower edge of the stag preserve where Prince was finishing his kill—giving Aurelie one sharp heartbeat of thinking *Eris set a trap for him*).
    2. Half a second later, **Eris's own amber bloodline ward along the highway monoliths shivers like plucked wire**—and the look on Eris's face (pure, glacial fury that someone used *his* warden-dispatch to track his column) proves to Aurelie in an instant that the ambush wasn't his.

### B. Pitch-Dark Proximity Under the Cliff Overhang
- **Killing the Fire:** With a single snap of his fist, Eris snuffs the gold campfire to black coal, plunging the Hidden Hollow into pitch mountain dark and cold starlight.
- **Tactical Deployment:**
  - **Caelum** ghosts up the east cairn with his longbow strung and twin hunting knives bare, russet fox ears flat as he tracks the bog-hounds turning off the paved highway into their draw.
  - **Forced Proximity in the Dark:** Bog-hounds track mortal heartbeats and sweat on the night wind. To keep her scent and sound sealed inside his personal blood-ward while the trackers probe the outer windbreak, **Eris backs Aurelie into the deep shadow beneath the granite cliff overhang**—wrapping his dark wool cloak around both of them, her spine to the cold rock, his chest flush against hers in the dark, one bare hand warm near her throat/jaw to feel if she makes a sound.
  - **The Friction:** She can feel the lethal heat coiling under his skin—not courtier polish, but a predator about to burn five men alive—while her bond with Prince screams that Prince is winging south at breakneck speed four miles away.

### C. The Breach & Midnight Skirmish (Keeping the Winter Twist Intact!)
- **Crucial Canon Guard:** Do **NOT** reveal Prince's ice-breath to Eris yet (Eris must keep believing dragons are fire-creatures until the Winter Court!). Prince kills with **dead-silent dive speed, crushing white-and-ice-blue weight, razor talons, and his golden tail-blade** (which shears open and shut like twin scimitars).
- **The Escalation:**
  - One bog-hound catches the residual ironwood/mortal trace at the split monoliths and bays; Vane's trackers loose a **red Forest House signal-bolt** or try to breach the outer stone windbreak to take a witness back to Beron.
  - Eris steps out of the overhang in full **Lucifer-Eris** lethality—no wasted words, pure silent white-gold ash-fire that turns wet ironwood and bog-hound collars to slag—while Caelum drops the horn-blower from the east cairn with an arrow through the throat.
  - **Prince's Midnight Drop:** Just as a mounted tracker wheels his horse to bolt back down the highway to warn Beron, **Prince drops vertically out of the starlit clouds onto the thirty-foot granite shelf / ravine mouth**—no roar until impact, golden tail-blade shearing clean through the escape path, azure eyes glowing in the dark above the cliff like twin cold stars.

### D. The Aftermath & Overnight Cost (Turn 7+ Payoff)
- **No Witnesses, New Stakes:** Now Eris knows Beron (or Kallum) has eyes on his southern dispatches—meaning the highway to the Summer border pass tomorrow is hot, and they cannot afford a single slip.
- **Physical & Emotional Edge:**
  - Option 1: Eris takes a cold-iron or ash-wood quarrel graze across his forearm/ribs when he intercepted a bolt meant for the shadow under the overhang (or burned a ward-net barehanded), and furiously refuses to make a sound about it back by the banked coals—forcing a razor-sharp tables-turned moment where Aurelie sees the blood on his cuff.
  - Option 2: With the outer ward burned out from the fight and Prince coiled on the thirty-foot granite shelf directly above them (close enough that his golden tail-blade hangs over the lip of the rock like a pendulum), Eris and Aurelie have to share the darkened canvas shelter on a half-banked coal bed for the rest of the watch while Caelum runs a two-mile sweep to erase the tracks.
