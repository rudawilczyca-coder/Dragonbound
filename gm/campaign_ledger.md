## 1. The Lucien Visit & His Unexpected Companion

### Who Comes With Lucien?
When Eris invites Lucien to Ashgrove to test Aurelie (and bait the Night Court's curiosity without triggering an open border incident), Lucien does **not** ride into the Autumn woods alone.
- **Candidate A (The Vassa / Jurian Wildcard — Band of Exiles):** **Lord Jurian** (or **Queen Vassa**, the cursed firebird queen in human form by day).
  - *Why Vassa is electric:* Vassa is a cursed human queen bound to a mythical bird of fire by Koschei's magic; Aurelie is a human noblewoman bound by a bargain mark to the Heir of Autumn and bonded to an ancient dragon. Vassa is sharp-tongued, aristocratic, takes zero nonsense from Fae lords, and immediately clocks that Aurelie is *not* a simple maidservant named "Lilly"—while Eris and Vassa despise/respect each other on sight like two apex birds circling the same carrion.
  - *Why Jurian is electric:* Jurian is an ancient mortal general who survived five hundred years as Amarantha's eye-in-a-ring, knows every dirty secret of High Fae history (including the old legends from before the Treaty when dragons vanished), and delights in getting under Eris's skin at the dinner table while casually dropping a cryptic historical clue about dragons and the Winter Mountains.
- **Candidate B (The Night Court Shadow):** **Azriel** (waiting unseen in the trees beyond the one-mile ward line, testing the perimeter where Prince's ancient magic hums against Eris's wards) OR **Mor** refusing to step foot in Autumn while sending a shadow-watched envoy.
- *GM Play Plan:* Have Lucien arrive at Ashgrove with **Jurian** (or **Vassa**—depending on which dynamic Ruby bites hardest on when the horses pull into the courtyard!). Better yet: Lucien brings **Jurian** inside the gates as his "human-realm liaison" from the manor on the continent/Velaris fringe, because Lucien heard a rumor that Eris acquired a *mortal girl from across the sea* and brought the one mortal in his circle who knows the eastern kingdoms' heraldry!
  - *The Live-Fire Trap:* Jurian takes one look at Aurelie's posture, her hands, and her phrasing, and realizes she is highborn Continental nobility (*Hightower*), forcing Aurelie and Eris into a razor-wire dance at the dinner table to keep her family's name out of Lucien's and Jurian's mouths!

## 2. The "Show Dinner" (Autumn Court Associates & Vultures)

### The True Purpose of the Dinner
Eris hosts four high-ranking Autumn nobles at Ashgrove under the guise of an autumn hunting supper:
1. **Lord Vastor of the Copper Marches** — Old-blood warlord who controls the eastern garrison roads; cynical, half-convinced Beron is past his prime, testing whether Eris has the stomach to kill his father or if Eris has gone soft in his country lodge.
2. **Lady Meridia of Redmount** — Silk-voiced poisoner and master of whispers in the eastern boroughs; openly allied with Eris's faction, **secretly taking gold from Beron's second son (Kallum)** to find out what the "white shadow" spotted over Ashgrove's ridge actually is.
3. **Lord Cassander** — Arrogant lesser lord who despises mortals and immediately treats "Lilly" the cupbearer as a toy to be humiliated—until Aurelie's razor-sharp "accidental" spill or barbed courtesy turns the table's laughter onto him, and later that night Eris deals with Cassander's insolence in private with lethal Autumn coldness.

### The Secret Hook at the Show Dinner
- While standing behind Eris's chair pouring wine, Aurelie will overhear a coded exchange between Lady Meridia and Lord Vastor about **a sealed iron vault under the Forest House**—and a shipment of Continental glass and chains that arrived from **Queen Ismena's ships** three months before Aurelie ever fell through the portal!
- *The Reveal Seed:* Queen Ismena of Hightower didn't find Dragon Egg #312 by accident. Someone in Prythian (or Koschei across the water, trading through Autumn intermediaries) knew what that petrified stone was before Aurelie ever touched it.

## 3. What Each High Lord Actually Wants (The Court Tour Matrix)

### I. Summer Court — High Lord Tarquin (Adriata)
- **What Eris tells Aurelie:** We are going to trade eastern timber tariffs for coastal shipping rights and leak a rumor to Helion's scholars.
- **What Tarquin actually wants (Hidden Hook):** Something has been hunting ships in the deep trenches south of Adriata—not sea wyrms, and not Hybern remnants, but cold currents radiating from the continental shelf. Tarquin's water-seers sense Prince's ancient pre-Treaty magic the moment they enter Adriata's palace and mistake Prince for an omen tied to the **Drowned Archive** beneath the Summer palace.
- **The Twist:** Tarquin offers Aurelie genuine warmth and sanctuary—not out of pity like Lucien, but because he believes a bonded dragon-rider shouldn't be leashed to Beron's heir. Yet Tarquin's cousin **Cresseida** quietly tries to bargain for a single feather or scale of Prince's shed plumage to power Summer's wards.

### II. Day Court — High Lord Helion Spellcleaver
- **What Helion actually wants (Hidden Hook):** Helion knows Eris and Lucien's deepest family secret (that **Lucien is Helion's biological son** by Lady Vanserra, and that Eris has protected his mother's secret for centuries at the cost of his own reputation).
- **The Dragon Codex Vault:** When Helion opens the pre-Treaty dragon vaults for Aurelie and Prince, the oldest codex is written in a dialect of the Old Language that Fae magic cannot untangle—only **Prince's breath or Aurelie's blood-bonded touch** can wake the ink.
- **What the Codex Reveals:** Dragons did not go extinct in Prythian by accident. At the end of the ancient wars, the last great flight of white-and-gold dragons retreated north into the **deepest glacial caverns of the Winter Court** and sealed themselves into stone sleep—waiting for a bloodline that could wake them.

### III. Winter Court — High Lord Kallias & Lady Viviane
- **Why Prince Demanded Winter After Summer (The Ancestral Pull):** Prince doesn't consciously remember everything in his blood yet—he is newly hatched, operating on ancient instinct—but in his marrow he feels the **resonating song of the Frost-Cradle** buried beneath the northernmost peaks of Kallias's territory.
- **What Kallias actually wants (Hidden Hook):** Kallias and Viviane fiercely distrust the Autumn Court (especially after the war and the border tensions). Worse, recent seismic tremors in the high glaciers—triggered from afar on the exact day Prince hatched and crossed into Prythian—have cracked the ice above the ancient dragon barrows. Kallias thinks Autumn is trying to wake a weapon under his mountains.
- **The Pressure Cooker:** In the freezing crystal halls of the Winter Court, Eris's fire magic makes him an immediate pariah—and for the first time, **Eris has to rely on Aurelie and Prince's ice-bright affinity** to keep them from being thrown into Kallias's dungeons, while sharing a single hearth-warmed suite in a palace of ice.
