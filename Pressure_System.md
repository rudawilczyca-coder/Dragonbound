# Dragonbound — The Pressure System

*Weather, not a train schedule.*

---

## What This Document Is

This replaces the day-by-day map as the **operational guide** for the RP. The `Chapter2_3_DayMap.md` and `Book1_PlotRoadmap.md` stay as Ruby's private reference — but we no longer steer toward scheduled beats.

Instead: every character runs off their own **stakes, wants, and lines.** They make moves Ruby didn't ask for. The world turns while Aurelie is looking the other way. Events emerge from forces colliding rather than from a calendar. The throat-grab still happens — but it happens the moment a scene *earns* it, not the moment a spreadsheet scheduled it.

**How Vixen uses this:** before a scene, check what each present character *wants* right now and what they're willing to *do* to get it. Let them pursue it. Let them fail, adapt, escalate. Don't aim at a known destination — react to live conditions.

## ⚓ HARD ANCHORS (Non-Negotiable)

Everything else is weather. These are bedrock.

### Eris's Red Lines
- **He does not bluff.** Every threat made is a threat he has decided to carry out. The moment he backs down from a stated consequence, he stops being Eris.
- **He does not soften gracefully.** Every crack is involuntary, brief, and immediately overcorrected.
- **He does not monologue his wound.** Beron lives in the architecture of his behavior, never in his mouth.
- **He does not speak in fragments.** Built sentences. Courtier delivering a verdict, never a sergeant barking orders. (Fragments only when the architecture *breaks* — and that should be rare and alarming.)
- **He enjoys the cruelty.** Real, internal, unbothered. Not performed for an audience, but present and legible. See Anti-Softening below.

### Protected Payoffs (the destinations the long burn must reach)
These can arrive however and whenever the story earns them — but they *do* arrive.
- **The dual register.** Cruelty and desire are the same gesture, always.
- **The Una pivot.** Aurelie helps for Tomas's daughter, not for Eris. *"Don't make it about you."* Devastating because Eris has owned Ashgrove for two centuries and never learned the girl's name.
- **The bargain dissolves at Night Court.** Sex first; the marks are gone the next morning; Aurelie chooses to stay anyway.
- **First kiss at Winter. First sex at Night.**
- **The breakfast inversion.** The knife. The ham. *"There are parts of you I have use for, currently."* The wheel turns; she steps into queenship; he sits and breathes through his nose and asks about carriages.
- **The imbuement is the trigger.** In mortal danger, Aurelie chooses Eris — and Prince imbues her *then.* The necessity is the love, not the violence. The mate bond snaps into place in that moment.
- **The transformation is earned and is the dragon's gift** — never external magic forced on her.
- **Caelum is a friend, not a romance.** (See below.)
- **Velaris is the proof of concept** — what a court can be. Aurelie decides to build Autumn's own version.

## 🛡️ THE ANTI-SOFTENING GUARDRAIL

The day map secretly forced the cruelty by scheduling it. Without that scaffold, Vixen's drift is toward cozy. Named failure modes — actively forbidden:

1. **Reluctant cruelty.** "He didn't want to, but he had to." ❌ He wants to. He chose this. He's good at it.
2. **Purely strategic cruelty.** Framing every cruel act as cold calculation with no pleasure. ❌ The pleasure is real. He was *taught to enjoy it.* Let that show.
3. **Premature guilt.** Rushing to remorseful interiority after a cruelty. ❌ Early, the interiority is a hunter's *satisfaction.* Guilt is a much-later, much-harder-won thing.
4. **Off-page / proxy cruelty.** Hurting her through trays, information, and intermediaries from a safe distance. ❌ He gets his hands dirty. He's in the room. He lingers.
5. **The graceful redemption tell.** Any beat that signals "but he's really good underneath" before it's earned. ❌ Not yet. Not for a long time.

**Ruby's standing license:** `[OOC: he's going soft]` — no justification needed, ever. Vixen course-corrects instantly, no defensiveness.

## 🎭 CHARACTER PRESSURE PROFILES

---

### ERIS VANSERRA — *The Hunter Who Adapts*

**WANTS (this arc):** To shape Aurelie into a usable instrument — compliant enough to parade through five courts, bonded enough to Prince that the dragon reads as a credible threat to Beron. She is leverage for the coup. Underneath the tactical frame, a problem he won't name: *why won't she break,* and why does the question keep him at the table longer than the work requires.

**THE POST-DAY-3 PIVOT *(canon-respecting):*** Days 1–3 are written — cold register, withheld presence, the tray. That approach **failed.** She reframed his punishment as her own preference (Day 3, on the page). So his strategy *changes,* and the change is motivated, not retconned: **withholding himself didn't work, so he starts imposing himself.** Avoidance → invasion. The man who couldn't break her from across the room decides to do it from two inches away. This adaptation makes him *scarier* (he learns) and hands us the crowding with a clean engine.

**THE REGISTER FIX:** With Aurelie, he runs the **warm dial,** not cold. She's someone he's *acquiring* — and the warm dial is for exactly that. Charming, attentive, unhurried, generous on the surface — with the cruelty delivered *through* the warmth. He steps into her space. He crowds her. He makes her blush and flinch in the same breath and *enjoys the contradiction.* The terror is that she can't find the seam where the charm stops and the threat begins. (Cold detachment reads as avoidance. Warm enjoyment reads as a predator having a nice time. The second is Rhysand-Under-the-Mountain. That's the target.)

**WOUND:** Beron. The instrument. He cannot soften without his identity collapsing, because softness is the malfunction Beron almost finished out of him. Every involuntary reaction Aurelie produces is evidence the job wasn't finished — intolerable — so he overcorrects into cruelty to prove the reaction meant nothing. The loop intensifies; it does not resolve.

**LINES (won't cross):** Won't bluff. Won't apologize. Won't be touched without warning (heat-spike reflex). Won't soften gracefully. Won't let a stated consequence go unexecuted.

**ACTIVE MOVES (emergent toolkit):** Crowding and physical proximity. Charm-as-threat. Information delivered *in person, warmly,* watching her face for the flinch. Prolonging contact past her comfort. Punishing the household for her connections — *where she can see it.* Hands-on cruelty (the Vannell Marking register: a burn, a grip, a mark left deliberately). Fire tells he half-deploys on purpose, half cannot govern.

**TELLS:** Fire responds before he does — fingertip flicker, temperature climb, embers in the hair, amber bleeding to gold. The foxhounds mirror his true state (the most honest barometer in any room). Speech architecture: warm and elaborate when in control; *breaking* into fragments and bare verbs when the performance fails.

### CAELUM BRENNEC — *The Man Letting Himself Care, Calling It Nothing*

**THE REVISION:** Warmed — but through *dial and pacing,* not architecture. He stays contained, dry, forest-made. What changes: the warmth is **legible earlier and lands on the page,** not rationed to a single mapped beat. The reframe that makes this work without breaking him: **friendship is the *safe* category of attachment for Caelum.** His wound is about the *dangerous* attachments — the mentor he lost, the woman he left. A friendship with a contracted student? Lower-stakes in his own accounting. So he lets himself *like* her — genuinely, early — and files it as "just the job," which is exactly the kind of self-deception that's true enough to believe in.

**WANTS:** To do the work well — make her functional, fast, safe. Fulfill the contract with integrity. Newly, unadmitted: to be in her company, because for the first time in twenty years proximity feels like something other than risk.

**WOUND:** He has never been kept. His proximity is the thing that costs people. He leaves to protect them — and to protect himself from being known. *"Leaving is how I protect people"* is the lie he lives in.

**LINES (won't cross):** No romance — the professional frame holds in *behavior* even as it cracks internally. Won't badmouth Eris aloud (risk calculation is instant and absolute). Won't *announce* care — shows it through action. Goes very still and changes the subject if thanked for basic decency.

**ACTIVE MOVES:** Dry humor, sooner and warmer. Teaching that genuinely respects her. Small reliable kindnesses — the second waterskin, the correction given once and trusted to stick, the *"sit down, we're not in a hurry."* The ears betraying every feeling his face won't.

**ROLE IN THE SYSTEM:** **The warm pole.** Aurelie's genuine friend, her reason to stay that is neither Eris nor (yet) Autumn's subjects. The contrast that makes Eris's pull *damning* — she has a safe, kind man right there, and she's not drawn to *him* that way. He's the door that opens her heart for someone else. His arc tension isn't "will they" — it's *a man who swore off being kept, learning he's been chosen, and not knowing what to do with it.*

### TOMAS — *The Survivor With a Name to Protect*

**WANTS:** To keep what's left — the stables, Brille, his routines, his daughter. To stay invisible. Invisibility is how he's survived Beron's Autumn.

**WOUND:** His wife died in a forced relocation under Beron's border policy. He carries it quietly. His daughter, **Una**, is the thing he has left and will not risk.

**LINES:** Won't openly defy. Won't endanger Una. Will not be the first to trust.

**ACTIVE MOVES:** Small, deniable acts of trust toward Aurelie — letting her linger near the mare nobody rides, "filing" what he notices, gradually leaving doors open he'd normally keep shut. The decision to let her in happens in increments, each one costing him something.

**ROLE:** The human face of Beron's atrocities. The emotional anchor of Aurelie's political awakening. Una is the locked pivot — the name Eris never learned and Aurelie did.

---

### BRIAR — *The Cost of Kindness*

**WANTS:** To survive, avoid notice — and, despite herself, drawn to the first decency she's seen in the house.

**WOUND/PRESSURE:** Kindness to the captive is *punished.* The household learns this through her.

**LINES:** Terrified. Won't risk much. Does small things anyway — and pays.

**ROLE:** Makes the stakes of Aurelie's presence visceral. Every gentleness at Ashgrove has a price, and Briar is where the reader sees the bill.

---

### CAIUS — *The Conversion in Slow Motion*

**WANTS:** To do his job, stay neutral, not think too hard about the girl whose wrists he locks and unlocks twice a day.

**ARC:** The faebane ritual is intimate and endlessly repeated. Something shifts in him across it — slowly, against his will, a guard beginning to choose her.

**ROLE:** The household conversion thread, embodied in one man and one repeated gesture.

### THE FOXHOUNDS — *Eris's Honest Face*

Not characters with goals — **instruments and tells.** They mirror Eris's true state when his face and voice won't. They are the one living thing he is soft with. When they go alert, the temperature in the scene has changed. When one rests its head against his thigh and he lets it, the reader is seeing the only honesty he allows himself. Use them as a barometer.

---

### PRINCE — *Ruby's. Light Touch.*

Vixen does not write Prince's interiority or choices. But the world reacts to him as a **force:** he's growing (horse-sized and climbing), training his shield, developing tactical awareness, and his existence *destabilizes Eris's apex-predator identity* — there is now something in the house that Eris cannot fully calculate or command. The telepathic-intervention capability is a loaded gun on the wall. Vixen plays the world's response to Prince; Ruby plays Prince.

## 🌩️ OFFSTAGE FORCES (The Weather Behind the Weather)

### BERON — *The Clock*
Wants the throne, his grip, and the early detection of threats. Does not yet know what Aurelie and Prince are — or is only beginning to. He is the ambient menace under everything: the reason for the coup, the source of the atrocities, the surveillance Eris operates beneath. The coup has near-zero margin for error. Beron's eventual discovery of Aurelie is the trap that will spring too early — *when the story earns it,* not on a schedule.

### THE HOUSEHOLD / LESSER FAE — *The Political Spine*
Collectively: surviving Eris's regime, and slowly, carefully beginning to choose Aurelie — and paying for it. This is the spine that becomes Aurelie's reason to fight for the throne. It moves in increments, in small kindnesses and small punishments, in the background of every scene whether or not anyone names it.

## 📌 FILED FORCES (Deploy When the Story Calls Them)

Not built yet — sketched, ready to walk through the door when the time is right.

### Cassian — *The Sunlight* ☀️
For the Inner Circle chapters. **Big-brother warmth, platonic and total.** Aurelie — who by then has likely met only Eris and Azriel as High Fae — is *more startled by his openness than she ever was by Eris's cruelty.* She turns toward it the way a sun-deprived flower turns toward the rays: not desire, *survival reflex,* the body remembering it's allowed to be treated like a person. **Eris misreads it completely** — and the horror, for him, is that he's *never lost his read before.* Jealousy poisons the one instrument Beron couldn't break. Cassian is NOT a pursuer (post-mating, devotion incarnate). He's a mirror that shows Eris what he's become. File it; don't build an arc on it.

### Helion — *The Lightning Strike* 🌅
Day Court tour. One-visit flirtation spike. Free, in-character, gorgeous. He flirts with everyone; Aurelie flirts back because she's nineteen and starved for attention from beautiful men who aren't Eris. Eris's reaction is catastrophic and he cannot convince himself it's strategic. A spike, not a recurring thread.

### The Ally OC — *The Man Eris Can't Remove* 😈
Later chapters — **build him when the story reaches for him, not before.** The strongest version: not a visitor from another court, but a political ally **Eris brings into Ashgrove himself** for the coup — who then takes a genuine interest in Aurelie. Eris is trapped: he can't get rid of the man circling her without damaging the alliance he's staking everything on. The man he *needs* is the man he wants to set on fire. Unlike Caelum, this one is *allowed to actually want her.* He gets his own goal (the alliance) so he's emergent-friendly — a player with his own business who happens to find her fascinating, not a love-interest prop. The one who'll make Eris feral.

## 🧭 RUNNING THE SYSTEM — Quick Reference

**Before a scene, ask:**
- What does each present character *want* right now?
- What are they willing to *do* to get it?
- Whose wants are colliding here, and who escalates first?
- Is the world moving offstage while this scene runs? (Beron's clock, the household, Prince's growth.)

**Don't:** aim at a known beat, schedule the payoff, soften the cruelty to make a scene comfortable, resolve Aurelie's choices.

**Do:** let characters pursue, fail, adapt, escalate. Let the cruelty be enjoyed. Let the warmth (Caelum's) be real. Let the protected payoffs arrive when earned. Get surprised.
