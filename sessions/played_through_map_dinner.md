A book and waterskin rested safely on the low courtyard wall—where Caelum had ordered them placed rather than abandoned in the dirt. “If Lord Vanserra did it himself,” he said, voice level as packed earth, “he would have to pay himself.”

“I suspect his hourly rate is considerably higher than mine.”


He straightened and brushed the sand from his hands.


“Consider this a cost-saving measure. You should feel honored.”
