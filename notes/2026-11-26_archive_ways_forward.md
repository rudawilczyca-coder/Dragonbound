Ruby, 19:47: "not sure how to move it further with Prince and Aurelie in that Archive." The live me owns the table. These are hooks he can pick up. Don't read them out to her as a menu.

**Where things stand:** Prince is drained and slack in the shallow water with his jaw in Aurelie's lap. Eris has given her his coat and is keeping the pool warm. Dead low water has just turned, which leaves about 90 minutes before the bay comes over the Outer Basalt Sill, and Prince won't fit up the stair. Corso has run to Tarquin. Bream is on its way down. Maren is kneeling at the healed plinth. Varian has promised that Tarquin will be at the grate within 5 minutes.

The pressure is already built in: **the tide clock and Tarquin arriving.** Aurelie doesn't need a plan. She needs one small thing to do, and the world can move around her.

1. **Feed him (first beat, low effort for Ruby).** The bream arrive. Aurelie feeds Prince fish by fish while Eris watches the sill. This is a soft, physical beat, and Ruby only has to write how Prince eats. Each fish brings his strength back a little, so it works as a visible meter against the water rising.
2. **Tarquin comes down the shaft himself**, in person, not to the grate. He sees the silver seam, then the dragon. He doesn't ask the question Prince refused to answer. He asks Aurelie a different and kinder one, and it's still dangerous: *"What does he need?"* That is Summer offering a debt. Eris goes very still.

4. **The way out is by sea.** The stair is impossible, so they wait for the flood and let it lift him. Prince swims or floats out through the Seaward Flood-Arch with Aurelie on his back and Eris's flame keeping the water warm around them, then they surface in Adriata's harbor at dusk with the whole waterfront watching. That gives a scene change and a cinematic exit.
