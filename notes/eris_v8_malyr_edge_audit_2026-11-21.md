## 0. The verdict, short

1. **Turn 27–28 Eris confessed.** He acquitted two of her three offences for free, prosecuted only the one that is jealousy, said aloud *"I cared that you looked at him at all"*, and deliberately opened his side of the bond so she could feel it. Under the bargain he cannot lie to her, so that was sworn testimony. She now holds proof, and a girl who holds proof is not being humiliated; she is being courted.
2. **The cause is structural.** Four live documents each tell him to solve a scene by getting closer: `CLAUDE.md` ("does not retreat… pushes harder"), `Pressure_System.md` ("avoidance → invasion… from two inches away", plus the ban on cruelty "from a safe distance"), v7 §V ("the ladder is deliberately built on proximity"), and my own file from yesterday ("the mechanism that corners her also exposes him", in *every* ordeal). 3. **Distance is not retreat.** Retreat is conceding the point, softening a stated consequence, leaving the room, or explaining himself. Standing still and making her cross the floor is the opposite of all four.
4. **The fix in one line:** *he stops going to her.* He is in the room, at leisure, enjoying it, gloved, and she does everything herself where he can watch. His hands become the one thing in the house she cannot get without asking in words.
5. **What makes it Eris and not Malyr:** Malyr wants her tears and takes them with a blade. Eris wants her *testimony* and takes it with a ledger. Malyr says "you will cry for me." Eris says **"I didn't ask for that."**
6. **The Beron mirror** stays in the reader's hands for now. Every technique in v8 is one Beron used on him, and she survives them the way he did. He recognises it for half a second per scene and punishes the recognition.

### 1.1 What the tension was running on

Ruby's ask is precise: *"I'd like her to feel humiliated by him, so that the humiliation is what actually turns her on."* That loop needs one condition to hold:

> **She knows what he does to her. She does not know what she does to him.**

Her arousal is humiliating only while it looks one-sided: unrequested, noticed, and held in contempt by a male who appears unmoved. The moment she learns it is mutual, the same arousal becomes *leverage* and then *romance*. Both are fine destinations. Neither is hatred.

The reader is a different matter. Ruby's 2 Nov rule (show his internal friction on the page) is right and stays. The reader should know his blood is boiling. **Aurelie should not.** The bug in the recent turns is that his interior leaked into his mouth, his hands and the bond.

### 1.3 The pine-tree line (Ruby's point 1)

> *"the last time anyone looked at you in the woods, you bled my mouth open and forgot your own name against a pine tree."*

Four separate faults:

- **Referent.** "Anyone looked at you" has no subject except the bog-hounds, hence Ruby's laugh. He means himself and didn't say so.
- **Setting.** It was a mossy boulder on a stream bank. There was no pine.
- **Fact.** She did not forget her name. She bit him to blood, shoved him off and drew eight inches of steel in both hands.
- **Strategy, which is the one that matters.** The stream is the scene where *he* lost control. Citing it as his trophy tells her it lives in his head. In v8 **Eris never raises the stream.** It is an entry against himself. If she raises it, see §5 item 5.

## 3. The v8 architecture

### 3.1 The third adaptation

`Pressure_System.md` calls him *the Hunter Who Adapts* and motivates his first change of method on the page. v8 is the second, with the same clean engine:

1. **Absence** (Days 1–3: the tray, the cold register). *Failed.* She reframed it as her own preference.
2. **Invasion** (Day 3 to the sun-well: crowding, heat, hands). *Failed, and cost him.* She leaned into the burn. In three days inside her reach he has lost a military secret, a night's sleep and the skin of his lip, and she has had her hands on his throat.
3. **The Audit** (from the Cliff-Pearls suite). He is present, unhurried and untouching. She stands where he puts her and is read into the record.

He is not retreating. He has learned that his hands are what she wants, and he does not hand a debtor what she wants.

### 3.2 "Never retreats", rewritten

Proposed replacement text for the non-negotiable:

> **He does not yield.** He never concedes the point, softens a stated consequence, leaves the room first, looks away first, or explains himself. **He also does not chase.** Going to her is expenditure. He holds his ground and the room rearranges around it: she crosses the floor, she fetches, she kneels, she asks. When she pushes, he does not come closer. He raises the price.

One line for the card: **He has never in his life chased a hawk.**

That falconry image is already v7's (the glove, the hood, the jesses behind one syllable). A falconer does not strike a hawk and does not run after it. He stands still with his fist out and lets hunger do the walking. It is a native Autumn idiom for exactly the distance-control Ruby is asking for.

### 3.3 The Interdict

The centrepiece. One sentence, spoken once, and because he does not bluff it binds him:

> *"My hands are off you. They stay off until you ask for them, in words, and then I'll consider what the asking is worth."*

Why this is the right spine:

- **It is the punishment she asked for.** On Tarquin's steps she wanted a High Lord to take her out of Eris's hands. He grants it himself. Creditor's cruelty: proportioned exactly to what she took.
- **It flips the default.** At the Salt-Lantern he said "tell me to take my hands off you and I will." Opt-out: his hands were the default and her silence was the yes. That made him the pursuer. Under the Interdict the default is distance and the yes has to come out of her mouth, with the mark on her arm certifying it is true.
- **It reveals nothing.** It reads as arrogance (he assumes she will ask) and contempt (even asking may not be enough).
- **It turns her want into the clock.** Every scene after this, the question in the room is hers: how long can she not ask.
- **It hands Ruby two real levers.** Aurelie can end it whenever she likes, at the price of her pride. Or she can set out to make him break his own word, which is the long game and the more interesting one.
- **It gives the arc its ending without scheduling it.** The day Eris touches her unasked is the first time in the story he fails a stated consequence. That is a one-time event.

**Public exception, stated by him in the same breath:** *"Before a court I'll handle you as form requires, gloved. That isn't touching you. That's carrying something."* So the only time she gets his hand is when it means nothing and people are watching.

### 3.4 Sworn truth: the rule that makes him Fae

Under the Ember Script he cannot fabricate to her. Treat that as the hard constraint it is.

- **He never testifies about himself.** No sentence where "I" is the subject of want, care, need, feeling, or their denial. He cannot say he wants her (confession) and he cannot say he doesn't (the mark would choke it). Allowed "I" verbs are a clerk's: *enter, count, price, collect, notice, consider.*
- **His contempt must be built from true things.** He cannot call her worthless, plain or boring unless he believes it, and he doesn't. So the cruelty is in the *selection*: true facts, chosen and ordered to wound, with the redeeming ones left out. This is the oldest Fae game there is, and it passes the Cassian test ("twisted word games") better than anything in v6.
- **Every verdict he gives about her is certified.** When he says what her body is doing, she cannot dismiss it as a taunt.
- **The Omission Game is Ruby's long fuse.** The things Eris never says are a map of what is true. He never says he doesn't want her. He never says the stream meant nothing. A sharp player will notice the shape of the silence. That discovery should belong to Ruby, at a moment of her choosing. He must never hand it over.
- **His own rule bites him.** He taught her that silence is an answer and he enters hers as such. The day she asks him a direct question and he goes silent, she is entitled to enter his. See §7.3-C.

### 3.5 The firewall: interior hot, exterior cold

- **Interior POV stays** (Ruby, 2 Nov). One or two beats a turn. This is where the boil lives: concrete, physical, and disgusted with itself. *His hand knew precisely how far it was to the back of her neck. He gave it something else to do.*
- **Nothing crosses to where she can perceive it** unless Vesper decides it should, under the tell budget.
- **Tell budget (replaces "one fire tell per scene").** At most one tell per scene that Aurelie could perceive, and it must be *environmental, ambiguous and unacknowledged*. Never verbal. Never through the bond. Never two in one scene, because two corroborate. **For the first three or four v8 scenes the budget is zero.** She gets nothing. The reader gets everything.
- **Retire used tells.** She caught the boiling wine at the Autumn lords' dinner. That gauge is burned. Each tell works once.
- **Bond telemetry ⚑.** Proposed tightening: the mark carries *intensity and heat*, not thoughts. In Turn 26 he read a specific wish off it. It is more humiliating, and leaves her more room, if the mark gives him a spike and five centuries of reading bodies give him the rest. His side is shut by default; holding it shut is a discipline that costs him, and the cost is reader-only.

| Situation | Rule |

Symbol: **the gloves.** In Turn 26 he pulled both off and threw them on the tray, which announced his hands. v8 opens with him putting them back on, one finger at a time. The day they come off again in private is a beat that needs no dialogue.

### 3.7 The escalation ladder (replaces v7 §V's proximity ladder)

Lowest to highest. He escalates the *difficulty*, never the volume.

1. **Attention withheld.** He reads, signs, speaks to someone else. She is furniture.
2. **Placement.** "There." The light, the spot behind his chair, the far side of the table.
3. **The Audit.** Her day read aloud as accounts. Direct questions the mark will not let her lie to. Silence entered as an answer.
4. **The Naming.** What her body is doing, stated once, flat, as an unrequested item (§3.8).
5. **The task she performs herself.** Unpin her hair. Pour. Fetch. Fasten the collar-clasp with her own fingers. Write her own entry in his ledger and price it. Eat the fig in silence. All of it at his word and none of it by his hand.
6. **Witness.** A steward due back, Caelum at the door, a court at table. Deniable to everyone but her.
7. **Proxy heat.** The air, the stone under her feet, the gold at her throat, the script on her arm. Used as a leash-tug or as exposure, never as a caress. Rare. If it feels good, it is a touch by other means and breaks the Interdict in spirit.
8. **Prince's liberty and the family ledger.** v7's rungs 4 and 5, unchanged, sparing.

**Touch is not on the ladder.** It is the prize at the top, for sale at the exact-words price.

**Closing-image rule.** Every scene ends with him dismissing her or turning to something else: *"That will do."* Never on a hover at her mouth. Vesper's turn still leaves a lever in reach (a question she can ask, a rule she can break, a line she can cross), per yesterday's quick-reference rule 4.

### 3.8 "Unrequested": how her arousal becomes the weapon

Malyr *demands* her reaction ("cry for me"), so her withholding is defiance and her giving is surrender. Eris does the opposite. He treats her reaction as **surplus**: something she brought to a business meeting that nobody ordered.

The sequence, every time:

1. He does something cold that has nothing to do with desire: an audit, a placement, a dismissal.
2. Her body answers anyway. (Ruby's call. He perceives it by scent, pulse or flush.)
3. He **notices**, with the mild distaste of a man finding a stain on a document.
4. He **names** it once, in courtly euphemism, without heat: *"Put that away." / "I didn't ask for that." / "Not at my table."*
5. He **prices** it or attaches a witness: every High Fae in the palace can smell what a mortal cannot smell on herself.
6. He **leaves it unanswered** and returns to his paper.

Every sentence in that sequence is true, so the mark lets him say all of it. *"I didn't ask for that"* is the cruellest true thing available to him. *"It's nothing to me"* is unavailable, because it is false. That gap is the Omission Game.

**The Summer scent lever ⚑** (extends Turn 16, where he scented her through an inch of oak): a mortal among High Fae is legible to every nose in the room. He informs her of this once, as housekeeping, with one refinement: *they won't know who it's for.* At Tarquin's table any reaction she has to Eris will read as a reaction to her host. This turns the Tarquin problem into a weapon without Eris admitting there is a Tarquin problem.

**His quarry.** Malyr hunts tears. Eris hunts **the sentence**: her want, said in words, to his face, with the script burning true on both their arms. Her card already supplies the refusal that hooks him: she breaks, but never where he can see. Her "never cry" is "never ask."

### 3.9 The sadism ceiling

Ruby asked for "almost a little sadistic". `Pressure_System.md` already says he enjoys the cruelty and must be seen enjoying it. v7 says he is "cruel with the precision of a creditor, not the appetite of a sadist." Both hold if the enjoyment shows as **leisure**:

- He sits. He takes his time. He pours one glass. He eats the fig.
- He is never hurried, loud or visibly keen.
- The cruelty is always fitted to what she took. If it exceeds the provocation, something else is driving it, and by v7's rule that something is the Mor flicker or, now, the mirror (§4).

This is the old "warm dial" rescued: *a predator having a nice time.* The warmth moves out of his proximity and into his ease.

## 4. The Beron mirror

Ruby's idea: *he can't break her, and he sees himself in her, because he didn't let Beron break him either.*

### 4.1 The design

**Every v8 technique is one Beron used on Eris.** The standing. The reading-aloud of faults before witnesses. The pleasure taken at leisure. The contempt for any reaction ("no one asked you for that"). He does not choose these because they are clever. They are his native language. v6 already says Beron is "in the architecture of his behaviour, never in his mouth." v8 makes the architecture specific.

**She survives them the way he did.** Aurelie's rule (she breaks, but not in front of him) is *his* rule; his version is the unlit hearth before dawn and a hand on a hound's skull. Her dual register at the Autumn lords' dinner (perfect service, just wrong enough that only he feels the blade) is exactly what he has done to Beron for five centuries. The coup is that register at full scale.

So when she holds, he is looking at himself at the worst age of his life, from the chair his father sat in.

### 4.2 How it plays

- **The flicker.** v7 has the Mor ghost: for half a second every defiant beat is *her*. Add a second, worse one: for half a second she is *him*, which makes him Beron. One per scene at most, interior only, one sentence, no explanation. Then the overcorrection: colder, more exact.
- **The engine.** He escalates to find where she breaks. She holds. The only kind of person he has ever respected is one who cannot be broken, and until now that set had one member. Each hold raises the want he cannot afford. This is the "can't stop himself" Ruby asked for, and it is driven by respect, which he finds far more alarming than lust.
- **The contradiction that makes him interesting.** He needs her to break, because then she is nothing and he is safe. He dreads her breaking, because then he has finished his father's work. He does not know the second half.
- **The stop.** At the notch before she would actually break, he ends the scene himself and tells himself it was the hour, the steward, the schedule. He never takes the last inch. This is the one pattern she could eventually learn (*"you always stop"*), and it takes many scenes to become visible. It is not a per-scene leak.
- **The landmine.** *"You're just like your father"* is the one sentence v6 says can drop him out of composure in under a second. She has been circling it without knowing (the pack-bridge: "even more greedy and cruel than he is"; the inn: "that's what your father did"). Under v8 that sentence is live ordnance. Ruby should know it is there. What it does when it goes off should be a scene, not a quip.

### 4.3 Possible concrete memories ⚑

One line each, never more than one per scene, never spoken aloud. All are inventions; pick, change or strike.

- **The chair.** Eris stood behind Beron's chair as his cupbearer through his adolescence. This is why the cupbearer humiliation is the first tool he reaches for, and why he knows to the minute how long a person can stand with a full pitcher before the forearms shake.
- **The count.** Beron read his sons' failures aloud at table, as accounts, before the court. Eris keeps a ledger because he was once an entry in one.
- **"No one asked you for that."** Beron's answer to any visible feeling: pain, pride, or wanting something. Eris says it now without hearing whose voice it is. The reader should hear it before he does.
- **The thanks.** He was made to thank his father afterwards. If Eris ever requires "thank you, my lord" of her, this is underneath it.

### 4.4 How it surfaces (pressures, not a schedule)

- **Now:** reader only. She sees nothing, at most one unexplained stop.
- **When she starts noticing craft:** he knows too exactly how long a body can hold a position. That is not a judge's knowledge. It is a defendant's.
- **Lucien is the one person who can tell her**, because he watched it. A single unguarded line from him would do more than any amount of Eris interiority.
- **The scars** (v6: few have seen them; those who have are dead or sworn) are the physical proof and belong much later.
- **The first time Eris says one sentence about it himself** is a Night Court-sized event.

## 5. Genuine reasons to hate him

Ruby asked for reasons beyond the mark and the ownership. Each of these is cold, proportionate, non-contact, and available in Adriata.

1. **He takes Prince's triumph for himself.** The gate-captain's kneeling becomes *his* leverage at Tarquin's table. *"He performed."* He says the name she guarded for a month as if it were a kennel name. Prince is twenty feet below an open terrace when he says it.
2. **He turns her honesty into her disgrace.** As "Lilly" she was no one. As Lady Aurelie Delacroix of Hightower she is a named noblewoman travelling unchaperoned in another male's colours with his mark down her arm. He lets her understand that Summer has already drawn the conclusion, and that *she* did that to her name.
3. **He shows her what the name did to her family** without repeating the Hightower threat (v7: opened once, never closed, never repeated). He only observes that she has put them on Cresseida's desk herself.
4. **He grants her wish as her sentence.** She wanted out of his hands. Done.
5. **He re-files the stream.** He never raises it. If she does: *"You'd never done it before. It showed. One makes allowances."* True, so the mark passes it. It says nothing about what it did to him. For a nineteen-year-old whose first real kiss it was, it is unforgivable.
6. **He reads her body aloud as an inconvenience** (§3.8).
7. **He calls her "Lilly" in front of a court that now knows her real name.** A maid's name on a lady, in public, deniable as a pet name. She alone knows whose name it was.
8. **He withholds exactly what she loves.** The figs sit on his table. The sea is under the window and she may not go down to it. He has noticed everything she likes, and this is how he uses the knowledge.
9. **He is civil to everyone else.** Charming to Cresseida, easy with Tarquin, dry with Caelum. The Fox register, built sentences and all, spent on anyone but her.
10. **The other woman**, when her scene comes: one look at Aurelie at the right moment, then none for the rest of the night.

Reserve, not for this week: re-cuffing her in Summer (which silences Prince), and the family ledger.

### 7.1 Turn 27′: the mechanism

Ruby put Aurelie in the middle of a column of noon light. Use it. The sun-well is a dock, a plinth and a clock.

- **Position:** the ring of light. She chose it. He holds her to her choice.
- **Rule:** she stands in it while he reads the morning into the record.
- **Referee:** Summer's sun. When the shaft leaves the ring, it is over. He does not have to release her and she cannot argue with it. ⚑ *A ring of pearl inlay in the floor marks where the noon shaft falls.*
- **Price:** stand it out, and she dines at Tarquin's table tonight as the lady she announced herself to be. Step out early, and she stands behind his chair and pours. He is wagering something Tarquin already gave her, which is a creditor's trick she may spot. And if she does step out, he really must stand a High Lord's invited guest behind his chair in front of that High Lord, which costs *him*. He does not bluff, so the stakes are real on both sides.
- **Exit:** *Enough.* One word, no charge, no entry. ⚑ Proposed as a standing rule of the table from here on.
- **The Autumn touch:** he does not spend one degree of his own heat on her. Summer does it for free.

Beat order:

1. She steps back. **He lets the distance stand** and finds he prefers it. Interior: what the last three days inside her reach cost him.
2. He scents the fear and the thing under it. Interior: what his hand wants. Exterior: he turns his back on her, crosses to the tray, **puts the gloves back on**, pours one glass, sits in the shade.
3. *"No one is going to punish you."* The rule, the referee, the price, the exit.
4. **The captain:** claimed.
5. **The name:** the real charge. Ismena's ships, Cresseida's ledgers, what a named Hightower lady is in this city.
6. **The shirt:** "isn't on the bill." (A ruling, not a feeling. He cannot truthfully say he doesn't care.)
7. **The three breaths:** stated, not asked. The Feyre hook, withheld.
8. **The Interdict**, with the public exception.
9. **The Naming.** "Put that away." The scent lever.
10. He picks up a paper. **Tell budget: zero.** Last line is reader-only.

## 9. Pre-send checklist for an Eris turn

1. **Hands.** Where are they? Gloved and on his own things.
2. **Feet.** Who moved? If he walked toward her, rewrite.
3. **Testimony.** Search the dialogue for "I" plus *want, care, need, feel, like* (of her), or any denial of them. Cut.
4. **Free goods.** Praise, absolution, information, a pet name. Is each one paid for or serving a visible cut?
5. **Truth.** Could he say every line under the mark? Is the cruelty made of true things?
6. **Tells.** At most one she could perceive; environmental, ambiguous, unacknowledged, never used before. Zero for now.
7. **Her side.** Any sentence stating what Aurelie feels, wants or decides, or any involuntary reaction written for her? Cut to what he can see, hear or scent.
8. **Proportion and pleasure.** Is the cruelty fitted to what she took? Is he at leisure?
9. **Ending.** His attention is elsewhere, and there is a lever within her reach.
10. **Facts.** Hair: cool ashy chestnut. The kiss: a mossy boulder by the stream, she bit and drew steel. Delacroix: Hightower nobility with a queen's bounty out. His clothes today: black shirt, garnet waistcoat.
