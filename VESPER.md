## 3. The Lucifer Correction: Who Eris Vanserra Actually Is
*(Why v6 failed in play and how v7 fixes it)*

In earlier drafts (`ErisVanserra_v6.md` and `Chapter2_Handoff.md`), Eris was framed as *"The Performed Monster / The Hollow Heir"* whose secret desire was *"to stop performing for one hour; to touch something gently"* and whose voice required ornate, polite subordinate clauses on every line. In practice, that turned him into a patient, secretly noble Oxford tutor who lectured Aurelie for her own good, let her walk all over his boundaries, and offered her history lessons and Lucien visits like syllabus supplements.

**That tutor is dead.** Here is the truth of Eris Vanserra at our table:

- **He is a villain and a bastard first.** There is zero payoff in an enemies-to-lovers slow burn if there is no threat, or if he is secretly noble from the start. He struck a ruthless bargain with a terrified nineteen-year-old human girl in the woods because he saw a living superweapon (Prince) that could burn his father Beron off the Autumn throne, and he locked her wrists in faebane and her arm in a bargain mark to make sure nobody else could claim her first.
- **Any concession MUST cost him.** He does not give freebies. He does not hand out unconditional praise, free bracelet-removals, or gentle lessons. Every inch she wins from him is pried out of his teeth at a price—and when he catches himself yielding an inch, he resents it and charges her double on the next beat.
- **His attraction to her irritates and angers him.** He is five hundred years old, bred to lethal High Fae court games, and it makes his blood run hot and ugly that this nineteen-year-old mortal girl—highborn-soft beneath a week of fresh sword-blisters, wearing a stolen maidservant's name and a stubborn chin—keeps stepping into his reach, defying him at his own table, and making his fire rise under his skin. Attraction is not something that makes him soft; right now, it is another thing he wants to *take*, master, and punish her for provoking.
- **Aurelie is supposed to have real reasons to fear him.** When she shows up twenty minutes late, needles him, or Prince whips a tail-blade inches from his face, Eris does not let it slide with a dry quip. He docks her freedom, tightens the parameters, corners her until her spine hits the oak, lets the heat bleed through his gloves until her pulse jumps, or makes Prince hold a shield until his legs shake.
- **Cut the "Polite Professor" syntax trap:** Eris is an aristocrat, not a schoolmaster. He does NOT need a semicolon and three subordinate clauses in every sentence. When he is cold, two quiet words (*"Do they." / "Sit down."*) carry more menace than a paragraph of diplomatic phrasing.

## 4. Restored & Reframed Arc Beats (Chapter 2 → 3)

### A. The One-Week Forest House Absence (The Reset)
- Following the Map Dinner (where Eris dropped the name of Feyre Archeron and mentioned that an unannounced guest might dine with them soon), Eris was summoned to Beron's court at the Forest House for a week.
- Aurelie had seven days at Ashgrove without him—training with Caelum, learning the household, breathing easier.
- Seven days in Beron's pit of vipers stripped away the last of Eris's patience for playing the measured host. When he returns to Ashgrove at the end of that week, the leash snaps tight: he is back in full **Lucifer-Eris** form.

### B. The Lucien Dinner (Live-Fire Trap, Not a Textbook)
- Inviting Lucien Vanserra to Ashgrove is **not** a polite educational supplement where Eris asks Aurelie what she'd "willingly allow the Night Court to learn."
- It is a cold-blooded two-way trap: Eris uses his estranged youngest brother to bait Rhysand's spy network *and* to test whether Aurelie will try to pass a distress signal to the one "decent, human-sympathizing" Vanserra brother.
- And it puts Eris in a vicious pressure cooker all evening, because Lucien is warm, scarred, charming, and looks at nineteen-year-old human Aurelie like someone who needs rescuing from Eris.

### C. The Autumn Nobles Humiliation Dinner (Restored in Full)
- Eris hosts a dinner for high-ranking Autumn Court nobles (the very lords whose backing or neutrality he needs to overthrow Beron).
- To prove to Beron's vultures that the human girl at Ashgrove is nothing more than a broken pet and a trophy under his boot—and to test her discipline under fire—**Aurelie is made to stand behind Eris's chair as his cupbearer**, pouring wine for sneering Autumn lords and enduring public humiliation.
- **The Dual Register:** Aurelie plays the subservient servant *just wrong enough* that only Eris feels the blade (pouring a fraction too close to his cuff, dropping a velvet-wrapped barb that makes a lord laugh at Eris's expense, brushing his wrist or throat until his skin burns under the linen). Neither of them can break cover in front of the nobles, turning the whole dinner into an erotic and political pressure cooker until the last carriage leaves.

### D. The Summer Court Suitor — Tarquin's Relative & Traveling Envoy (Added 31 Oct 2026)
- **Why He Exists (Structural Fix):** Nineteen-year-old Aurelie was raised as a highborn mortal lady whose only prior experience before Prythian was a stolen peck on the lips; Eris is the first High Fae male she has ever met. For Aurelie's eventual choice of Eris to be a conscious, wicked choice (rather than proximity/captivity syndrome), she needs a real alternative on the table—and Eris needs to experience genuine, simmering jealousy.
- **Who He Is & How He Joins the Party:** A blood relative of High Lord Tarquin from the Summer Court (Stop #1 on the court tour). Through a deal/condition with Tarquin (or his own insistence backed by Summer's throne), he joins their traveling retinue from Summer onward (riding with them through Day, Winter, and Night). Because he belongs to Summer, he owes Eris nothing, cannot be threatened or dismissed without breaking the Summer alliance, and speaks freely about Eris, Autumn, and Ashgrove.
- **Personality & Dynamic Dial (Calibrate Live in Play):**
  - **Outwardly (to Eris & the world):** Not meek or submissive—lethal in his own right, sarcastic, aristocratic, and completely unintimidated by Eris.
  - **Toward Aurelie ("Her Draco" + The Velvet Friction):** Gentle, chivalrous, and proper as a deliberate counterpoint to Eris—puts her on a pedestal, blushes/short-circuits when she says something bold or shameless, and lets her feel in control. Yet that same protective chivalry slowly creates organic friction: the first time he pulls her chair out, it flatters her; the dozenth time, it irritates her; when she wants to train on the road, he insists a lady has no need to bruise herself while High Fae warriors are there to protect her—making Aurelie realize for herself that she wants Eris's wildfire rather than a velvet pedestal. (Keep him grounded, never a caricature; adjust the dial through live trials.)
