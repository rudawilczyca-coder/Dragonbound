## I. Facts
Name: Aurelie Delacroix
Age: 19
Species: Human (pre-Prythian)
Origin: South mortal lands, she's nobility and was set to marry the son of one of the Six Mortal Queens
How she arrived in Prythian: She received a petrified dragon egg which hatched, she ran away, got on a ship and sailed for Prythian she is currently on her way to cross the wall.
Current situation: refugee, alone
## II. Physical Appearance
Height / build: 165cm / plump but no chubby - the body of someone who was never lacking food and was allowed physical movement though never trained in combat
Hair: ruddy-brown, shoulder-length, slightly wavy, has a sideways bag
Eyes: steel-silver
Distinguishing features: a mark on her left palm in the shape that vaguely resembles a dragon coiled like a letter "e" - the mark of dragon bond ; other than that her body is pristine
How she carries herself: defiant, chin raised high, she's not used to bowing to people, though when she is in hiding she tries to make herself look smaller. With all her defiance, she is easy to startle / embarrass.
What doesn't match: I'm not sure how to describe it but she feels much better when people are not watching her. The performance of nobility tires her.
## III. The Dragon
Name: Princeth Dia Yang (Prince for short)
Visual: White and ice-blue, feathered wings with membrane sections, golden tail blade (opens/closes), golden horns, azure blue cat-like eyes
Current size: small cat-sized (still fits in Aurelie's travel backpack) - 10 lbs
Bond: They exchange glances, like there are words passing between them. He often seeks her physical contact and reassurance. Likes perching on her shoulder (though he's almost too big for it now).
What NPCs notice: the ancient/regal quality even at small size, Fae instinctively respond to him with deference, his eyes are azure blue, cat-like, and the irises shrink when he doesn't like someone/something, he has an aura of magic about him though it's wild and untamed
## IV. Observable Behaviour
Default demeanour: Exactly like what she is - a spoiled girl forced into hiding enduring the road and hardships of a commoner for the first time
Under pressure: She gets harder, the more someone presses her negatively the more defiant she gets. She gets off on this. If the pressure is charged/physical though she folds.
Around authority: She can identify power structures at glance, she knows when and whom to bow even if it doesn't come easily to her.
Around kindness: It startles her, because it was utterly absent in her life until now.
Physical habits / tells:
(for NPCs to observe and react to, NOT for the bot to interpret) Twirling her hair when nervous, biting on her lip when deep in thought
## V. What She Doesn't Know
About Prythian: She knows that Fae have magic. She knows that there are courts. And that would be about it.
About the bond: She knows a dragon in human lands would earn a death sentence or be treated like a captive plaything. She suspects dragons are rare in Prythian as well, though she hopes there are some and she will be able to deliver the hatchling to its kind. She slowly realizes she might not want to part with Prince.
About herself: That the dragon hatching saved her from a completely boring existence of a princess-broodmare. That she'd much rather die free than live tied in shackles.
