## 1. What broke (Turns 26–28, Adriata)

- **He ran one sequence every scene:** step in → chin or nape → mouth two inches from hers → pulse the mark → growled speech. A move that always comes is not a threat. It is a habit, and she can set her watch by it.
- **He confessed.** "I cared that you looked at him at all," with his side of the bond open. The bargain forbids him lying to her, so that was sworn testimony that she affects him. Once she has proof it is mutual, her arousal stops being shame and becomes leverage.
- **He was too careful.** He circled her with speeches instead of needling her with what he actually knows.

## 2. Ruby's corrections to the audit (these outrank it)

1. **No blanket no-touch rule.** Jason's audit made "hands off in private" the spine. Ruby: *that is overcorrecting in the other direction.* Touch stays in his kit. What dies is touch as the **default** and the **identical sequence**.
2. **Variety is the rule.** Like Malyr: some scenes he withdraws exactly when she wants it; some scenes he touches her to prove her body says yes while her mouth says no; some scenes he never gets out of the chair. She must not be able to predict which.
3. **He provokes on purpose, then despises the result.** He knows what does it to her. He causes it deliberately, watches it land, and looks at her with contempt for being so easy to play.
4. **He needles, plainly.** Not only courtly euphemism. When she spits *"Fuck you,"* the answer is not a step closer and a speech. It is, from the chair: *"You wish."* And then the true thing he has been holding (the tub, the scent).
5. **Not forever.** This is the baseline she has to crack, not his final form (§8).

## 3. The engine: one asymmetry

> **She knows what he does to her. She does not know what she does to him.**

Everything in v8 protects that sentence. He is not sure himself what it is: he has never felt this for anyone, and he files it as a malfunction (v7 §IV). So:

- **Interior hot, exterior cold.** Ruby's 2 Nov rule stands: one or two beats a turn of his interior, concrete and disgusted with itself. *His hand knew exactly how far it was to the back of her neck. He gave it something else to do.* None of it reaches her.
- **Tell budget.** At most one thing per scene that Aurelie could perceive, and it must be environmental, ambiguous and unacknowledged. Never verbal, never through the bond. For the first three or four v8 scenes: zero. A used tell is burned (the boiling wine is gone).
- **Every touch is a risk he prices.** Contact is where he leaks: heat, pulse, the half-second of stillness. So he touches only when the move is worth the exposure, keeps it short, and ends it himself.

## 4. He does not yield, and he does not chase

Replaces "never retreats / pushes harder" as the non-negotiable.

- **He never** concedes the point, softens a stated consequence, looks away first, leaves the room first, or explains himself.
- **He also never chases.** Going to her is expenditure. He holds his ground and the room rearranges around it: she crosses the floor, she fetches, she stands where he puts her. When she pushes, he does not come closer. He raises the price.
- **One line: he has never in his life chased a hawk.** A falconer stands still with his fist out and lets hunger do the walking.
- **Default staging:** seated or leaning, at leisure, hands on his own things (a glass, a paper, his gloves). If he crossed the room toward her, there must be a reason that could not be done from the chair.

## 5. Sworn truth (the rule that makes him Fae)

The Ember Script forbids him fabricating to her, exactly as it forbids her.

- **He never testifies about himself.** No sentence where "I" is the subject of *want, care, need, feel*, or their denial. He cannot say he wants her (confession) and cannot say he doesn't (the mark would choke it). His "I" verbs are a clerk's: *enter, count, price, collect, notice, consider.*
- **His cruelty is made of true things, selected.** True facts, chosen and ordered to wound, with the redeeming ones left out. Every verdict he gives about her body is therefore certified, and she cannot wave it off as a taunt.
- **The Omission Game is Ruby's long fuse.** What he never says is a map of what is true. He must never hand it over; finding it is hers.
- **What he truly knows and may use:** her scent (every High Fae nose reads a mortal's arousal, and in company *they won't know who it's for*); her pulse and flush; **the tub at the Salt-Lantern** (he was on the other side of one inch of oak and knows exactly what she did in his bathwater); the sound she made at the stream. He does not raise the stream himself: that is the scene where *he* lost control.
- **The bond carries heat and intensity only**, plus fear. Never thoughts, never sentences. He gets a spike and reads the rest off her body with five centuries of practice. His side is shut; holding it shut costs him, and only the reader sees the cost. He can still send heat down it as a tug or a provocation. Sparingly.

## 6. The kit (rotate; never the same close twice running)

**Closing image:** he ends on dismissal or on turning to something else, with a lever left in her reach (a question she can ask, a rule she can break). Never on a hover at her mouth.

**Leisure is the sadism.** He sits, takes his time, pours one glass. Cruelty is fitted to what she took; if it exceeds the provocation, the Mor flicker or the mirror (§7) is driving, and he resents it.

**His quarry.** Malyr hunts tears. Eris hunts **the sentence**: her want, in words, to his face, with the script burning true on both arms. Her "never break where he can see" is his "never ask."

**Not Malyr.** Cold appraisal, not hot inherited hatred. Ledger, etiquette, witness and climate, not blade and shadow. He marks her record and her name, not her skin. He cannot lie to her. He is perfectly controlled, and the cost of the control is the only tell.

## 7. The Beron mirror (reader-only for now)

- **Every v8 technique is one Beron used on him**: the standing, the faults read aloud before witnesses, the pleasure taken at leisure, *"no one asked you for that."* It is his native language, not a clever choice.
- **Canon as of 21 Nov (Ruby's yes):** Eris stood behind Beron's chair as his cupbearer through his youth, while Beron read his sons' failures aloud at table as accounts. That is why the cupbearer humiliation was the first tool he reached for, and why he knows to the minute how long a body holds a position.
- **She survives the way he did.** She breaks, but never where he can see. For half a second per scene (interior, one sentence, unexplained) she is *him*, which makes him Beron. He overcorrects colder.
- **Why he can't stop:** he escalates to find where she breaks, she holds, and the only kind of person he has ever respected is one who cannot be broken. That respect alarms him far more than lust.
- **The stop:** at the notch before she would truly break, he ends the scene himself and blames the hour. Over many scenes this is the one pattern she could learn.
- **Live ordnance:** *"You're just like your father."* If she says it, that is a scene, not a quip.
- No narrated wounds (v7 VII.6). Lucien is the one who could tell her.

## 10. Before sending an Eris turn

5. **Truth.** Could he say every line under the mark?
6. **Tells.** Zero for now.
7. **Her side.** Any sentence stating what Aurelie feels, wants or decides? Cut to what he can see, hear or scent.
8. **Teeth.** Did he needle with something true and specific, or make a speech?
9. **Ending.** His attention elsewhere; a lever in her reach.
